## 3. Content Pillars (5 Pillars)

### Pillar 1: AI Magic (30%)
Showcase the AI diagnostics feature. Before/after analysis, speed demos, surprising identifications. This is your "wow factor" content.

### Pillar 2: Moto Knowledge (25%)
Educational motorcycle content — quick tips, explainers, "did you know" facts. Positions MotoVault as the authority.

### Pillar 3: Product Showcase (20%)
Clean app screenshots, feature walkthroughs, UI close-ups. Show the beautiful interface and UX.

### Pillar 4: Community & Social Proof (15%)
Beta user quotes, download milestones, rider stories. Builds trust and FOMO.

### Pillar 5: Brand & Culture (10%)
Behind-the-scenes, the team, moto lifestyle imagery. Humanizes the brand.

## 4. Visual Identity Rules

### Color Palette (from design system)
- **Primary background**: Dark Navy `#0F1B2D` (hero cards, feature posts)
- **Signature accent**: Exhaust Copper `#D4622E` (CTAs, highlights, emphasis)
- **Brand blue**: `#3366E6` (secondary accent, links, tech elements)
- **Teal green**: `#2D9E78` (success states, positive messaging)
- **Warm gold**: for encouragement/achievements
- **Text on dark**: White `#FAFAFA`
- **Light cards**: `#F5F5F5` background with `#171717` text

### Typography
- **Headlines**: Plus Jakarta Sans Bold (700)
- **Body text**: Plus Jakarta Sans Regular (400)
- **Accent text**: Plus Jakarta Sans SemiBold (600)
- Use the app's actual font in all graphics for consistency

### Grid Strategy
Alternate between dark-background posts (navy) and light-background posts (white/light gray) to create a **checkerboard rhythm** in the grid. This gives the profile a polished, intentional feel without requiring strict row themes.

### Post Dimensions
- Feed posts: 1080×1080 (square) or 1080×1350 (portrait, preferred for reach)
- Stories: 1080×1920
- Reels: 1080×1920
- Carousel slides: 1080×1350 (all same size)

## 6. Caption Framework

### Structure
Every caption should follow this pattern:

```
[Hook — first line that grabs attention]

[2-3 lines of value or context]

[CTA — ask them to do something]

[Hashtags — in first comment, not caption]
```

### Primary Hashtags (use on every post, rotate sets)

**Set A (moto community):**

**Set B (learning/tech):**
#motorcycletips #learnmotorcycle #biketech #motorcyclediagnostics #AItech #motorcycleknowledge #diymotorcycle #wrenchlife

**Set C (brand-specific):**
#motovault #motovaultapp #learnyourbike #fixyourbike #motorcycleapp #smartrider

### Rules
- Use 15-20 hashtags per post (in first comment, not caption body)
- Mix 1 set of community tags + 1 set of niche tags + brand tags
- Rotate sets to avoid shadowban signals
- Research and replace underperforming tags monthly

## 8. Engagement Strategy

### Daily Actions (15 min/day)
- Reply to every comment within 2 hours
- Like and comment on 10 posts in #motorcyclemaintenance and #bikelife
- Engage with 5 accounts in the moto-tech/DIY space
- Reply to all DMs same day

### Weekly Actions
- Run 1 poll or question sticker in Stories
- Share 1 piece of UGC (user-generated content) or repost a tagged story
- Follow 20-30 relevant accounts in the motorcycle niche

### Growth Tactics
- Collaborate with small moto creators (1K-10K followers) for feature swaps
- Comment thoughtfully on large moto accounts — be helpful, not spammy
- Use the "AI diagnosis" wow factor as your viral hook

## 9. Posting Schedule

### Optimal Times (motorcycle audience, general US/EU)
- **Weekdays**: 7:00 AM, 12:00 PM, or 6:00 PM (local time)
- **Weekends**: 9:00 AM or 5:00 PM
- Adjust based on Instagram Insights data after 2 weeks

### Frequency
- **Feed posts**: 4-5 per week
- **Stories**: Daily (at minimum 3/week)
- **Reels**: 1-2 per week
- **Carousels**: 1-2 per week (highest save rate)
