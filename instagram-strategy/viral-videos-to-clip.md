# Viral Motorcycle Videos to Clip into Shorts
## For @motovaultglobal via [Opus Clip](https://clip.opus.pro/dashboard)

> **How to use this list:** Paste each YouTube URL into Opus Clip. In the **"Include specific moments"** field, paste the ready-made prompts below. Each video has multiple prompts — use one per clip run, or combine them for a batch.

> **Prompt format (ClipAnything rules):**
> - Focus on **subject + action + visual details**
> - Describe the **moment you want**, not what you don't want
> - Don't request edits, effects, length, or format — just describe the scene
> - Keep prompts in English

> **Important:** These are third-party videos. Use Opus Clip's output as **inspiration and reference** for your own branded shorts, or repost with proper credit/permission.
