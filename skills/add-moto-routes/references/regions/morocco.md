### Tizi n'Tichka & Draa Valley — Marrakech to Zagora
Meta: MA | MA-05 | Marrakech | 2d | 440km | moderate | paved | 3400m↑ | pick:yes
Start: 31.6295, -7.9811
Description: Two days crossing the High Atlas and descending into the pre-Saharan Draa Valley. The N9 climbs to Tizi n'Tichka (2,260m) — the highest pass in the High Atlas — then drops to the oasis city of Ouarzazate. Day 2 follows the Draa river through 100km of palm oases and kasbahs (earthen fortresses) to Zagora, where the Sahara begins.

Waypoints:
1. [start | d0 | morning] Marrakech (31.6295, (-7.9811)::numeric) — Start in Marrakech's Jemaa el-Fnaa square. Head south on the N9. Fuel up at the city edge.
2. [scenic | d0 | morning] Taddert Village (31.4000, (-7.5167)::numeric) — The last village before the pass summit. Roadside argan oil sellers and carpet merchants. Views back to Marrakech plain.
3. [pass_summit | d0 | morning] Tizi n'Tichka Pass (2,260m) (31.2667, (-7.3667)::numeric) — Highest pass in the High Atlas. The Atlas range stretches endlessly in both directions. Fossils and minerals sold at summit stalls.
4. [scenic | d0 | afternoon] Aït Benhaddou (31.0472, (-7.1278)::numeric) — UNESCO World Heritage ksar (earthen castle village). Used in countless films: Gladiator, Game of Thrones, Lawrence of Arabia. 2km off the main road.
5. [overnight | d0 | evening] Ouarzazate (30.9189, (-6.9031)::numeric) — Overnight in Ouarzazate — the 'door of the desert' and Morocco's film capital. Studios here have produced 400+ films.
6. [scenic | d1 | morning] Agdz (30.6944, (-6.4536)::numeric) — The Draa Valley begins here. A narrow ribbon of green palms between barren rocky hammadas (plateaus).
7. [photo | d1 | morning] Draa Valley Palm Oasis Viewpoint (30.5000, (-6.0833)::numeric) — The road runs through 100km of continuous palm oasis. A green river flowing through brown desert.
8. [scenic | d1 | afternoon] Tamegroute (30.2681, (-5.6694)::numeric) — Library with 4,000 Islamic manuscripts dating to the 13th century. Green-glazed pottery made here.
9. [end | d1 | afternoon] Zagora (30.3272, (-5.8386)::numeric) — End in Zagora. The famous sign reads '52 days to Timbuktu by camel'. The dunes of Erg Chegaga are 90km further south by piste.

### Todra & Dadès Gorges Circuit
Meta: MA | MA-08 | Ouarzazate | 1d | 250km | moderate | paved | 2200m↑ | pick:yes
Start: 30.9189, -6.9031
Description: A circuit through Morocco's two most famous gorges from Ouarzazate. The Dadès Gorge is famous for its bizarre rock formations and the village of Aït Benhaddou at its mouth. The Todra Gorge narrows to just 10m wide while rising 300m on both sides — one of the most dramatic slot canyons anywhere in Africa.

Waypoints:
1. [start | d0 | morning] Ouarzazate (30.9189, (-6.9031)::numeric) — Start in Ouarzazate. Head east on the N10 toward the gorges.
2. [scenic | d0 | morning] Skoura Oasis (30.9167, (-6.5667)::numeric) — 1,000-year-old palm oasis with kasbahs rising from the palms. Amerhidil Kasbah is the most photogenic.
3. [scenic | d0 | morning] Dadès Gorge — Monkey Fingers Rocks (31.5167, (-6.0500)::numeric) — The famous eroded rock formations at the gorge's lower end look like giant fingers. The road climbs through them.
4. [pass_summit | d0 | morning] Dadès Gorge Upper Road (31.5500, (-5.9000)::numeric) — The upper gorge narrows and the road becomes seriously twisty. 26 hairpins in quick succession — Morocco's most exciting mountain road.
5. [food | d0 | afternoon] Tinerhir (31.5178, (-5.5228)::numeric) — Lunch in Tinerhir oasis town. Head north on the R703 to the Todra Gorge.
6. [photo | d0 | afternoon] Todra Gorge Slot Canyon (31.5700, (-5.5700)::numeric) — The gorge narrows to 10m at its tightest point. Cliff walls rise 300m vertically on both sides. Rock climbers hang from the walls above you.
7. [scenic | d0 | afternoon] Todra River Valley (31.5000, (-5.5500)::numeric) — Above the gorge the valley opens to terraced Berber villages beneath the High Atlas. The contrast with the gorge below is dramatic.
8. [end | d0 | afternoon] Ouarzazate (30.9189, (-6.9031)::numeric) — Return to Ouarzazate via N10.

### Rif Mountains — Fez to Chefchaouen
Meta: MA | MA-04 | Fez | 1d | 200km | moderate | paved | 1800m↑ | pick:no
Start: 34.0181, -5.0078
Description: The Rif Mountains route from the imperial city of Fez to the blue-painted hilltop village of Chefchaouen. The road crosses the Middle Atlas and climbs through cedar forests where wild Barbary macaques come to the roadside. Chefchaouen is famous worldwide for its blue-washed medina perched on a hillside below two peaks.

Waypoints:
1. [start | d0 | morning] Fez (34.0181, (-5.0078)::numeric) — Start in Fez — Morocco's spiritual and cultural capital. The Fes el-Bali medina is the world's largest car-free urban area.
2. [scenic | d0 | morning] Ifrane (33.5275, (-5.1053)::numeric) — Swiss-style alpine town at 1,665m. Called 'Little Switzerland'. Lion of Ifrane stone sculpture in the central square.
3. [scenic | d0 | morning] Azrou Cedar Forest (33.4378, (-5.2178)::numeric) — Ancient Atlas cedar forest. Barbary macaques (apes) sit on the road and will climb onto your bike for food. Fun but cautious.
4. [food | d0 | afternoon] Midelt (32.6839, (-4.7344)::numeric) — The 'apple capital' of Morocco at 1,500m. Lunch here. The Cirque de Jaffar mountain circuit nearby is spectacular.
5. [scenic | d0 | afternoon] Chefchaouen Approach (35.1200, (-5.1800)::numeric) — The final climb to Chefchaouen through Rif hill country.
6. [end | d0 | afternoon] Chefchaouen (35.1688, (-5.2688)::numeric) — End in Chefchaouen — the Blue City. Every wall, staircase, and doorway painted in shades of blue. Extremely photogenic and very cool.

### Anti-Atlas — Taroudant to Tiznit Coast Road
Meta: MA | MA-09 | Taroudant | 1d | 180km | moderate | paved | 1500m↑ | pick:no
Start: 30.4706, -8.8756
Description: The Anti-Atlas mountain range between Taroudant and the Atlantic coast via the Tizi Mighert pass. Less-visited than the High Atlas, these mountains have a rugged Berber character — argan orchards, saffron villages, and ancient granary buildings (igoudars) perched on clifftops.

Waypoints:
1. [start | d0 | morning] Taroudant (30.4706, (-8.8756)::numeric) — Start inside Taroudant's magnificent pink-ochre ramparts. Known as 'little Marrakech'. Head south on the R105.
2. [scenic | d0 | morning] Tizi Mighert Pass (1,697m) (29.9500, (-8.5500)::numeric) — The Anti-Atlas crossing. Views north to the High Atlas and south to the pre-Saharan plain.
3. [scenic | d0 | morning] Tafraout Boulders (29.7233, (-8.9767)::numeric) — Rose-coloured granite boulders of unusual shapes piled around a Berber village. The Belgian artist Jean Vérame painted many of them blue in 1984.
4. [photo | d0 | afternoon] Painted Rocks (29.7000, (-8.9500)::numeric) — The vivid blue and red-painted boulders from the 1984 art installation. Controversial but genuinely striking in the landscape.
5. [food | d0 | afternoon] Tiznit (29.6978, (-9.7364)::numeric) — Silver jewellery capital of southern Morocco. Good lunch at the central square.
6. [end | d0 | afternoon] Agadir (30.4278, (-9.5981)::numeric) — End in Agadir on the Atlantic coast. Beach resort after the mountains.
