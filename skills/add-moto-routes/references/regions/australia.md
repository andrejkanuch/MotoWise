### Great Ocean Road — Torquay to Warrnambool
Meta: AU | AU-VIC | Torquay | 2d | 430km | moderate | paved | 2400m↑ | pick:yes
Start: -38.3374, 144.3238
Description: Australia's most iconic coastal road hugs the Southern Ocean cliffs for 243km of Great Ocean Road before continuing to the Shipwreck Coast. Built by returned WWI soldiers as a memorial, it passes surf beaches, rainforest gullies, and ends at the Twelve Apostles — limestone stacks being slowly consumed by the Southern Ocean. Drive east-to-west for the best light on the Apostles.

Waypoints:
1. [start | d0 | morning] Torquay ((-38.3374)::numeric, 144.3238::numeric) — Start at Torquay, surf capital of Australia. Visit the Surfworld museum then head southwest on the B100.
2. [scenic | d0 | morning] Lorne ((-38.5405)::numeric, 143.9838::numeric) — First major Great Ocean Road town. Waterfalls in the Otways hinterland just inland. Best coffee on the coast.
3. [photo | d0 | afternoon] Cape Patton Lookout ((-38.7100)::numeric, 143.7300::numeric) — The road climbs high above the sea here — one of the GOR's most dramatic cliff-top viewpoints.
4. [overnight | d0 | evening] Apollo Bay ((-38.7558)::numeric, 143.6719::numeric) — Overnight in Apollo Bay. Working fishing town with excellent fresh crayfish. Quieter than Lorne.
5. [scenic | d1 | morning] Otways Rainforest — Maits Rest ((-38.7367)::numeric, 143.5667::numeric) — Ancient temperate rainforest walk, 30 minutes. Towering myrtle beech and tree ferns. Cool and mossy.
6. [photo | d1 | morning] Twelve Apostles ((-38.6643)::numeric, 143.1045::numeric) — The centrepiece of the Great Ocean Road. 8 of the original 12 stacks remain. Sunrise or late afternoon light is best. Helicopter tours operate above.
7. [scenic | d1 | afternoon] Loch Ard Gorge ((-38.6809)::numeric, 143.0581::numeric) — Named after the clipper ship that wrecked here in 1878. Only 2 survived. Beautiful sheltered beach inside the gorge.
8. [food | d1 | afternoon] Port Campbell ((-38.6108)::numeric, 142.9947::numeric) — Lunch in Port Campbell. Small, friendly town with a good pub.
9. [end | d1 | afternoon] Warrnambool ((-38.3827)::numeric, 142.4877::numeric) — End in Warrnambool. Whale watching May–October (Southern Right Whales calve at Logans Beach).

### Great Alpine Road Victoria — Wangaratta to Bairnsdale
Meta: AU | AU-VIC | Wangaratta | 1d | 305km | moderate | paved | 2800m↑ | pick:yes
Start: -36.3576, 146.3136
Description: Australia's highest sealed road traverses the Victorian Alps from the Murray Valley to the Gippsland coast. The B500 climbs through the wine country of the King Valley, ascends the ski resort of Falls Creek (1,780m), crosses Hotham (1,750m), and descends through wild mountain ash forest to Bairnsdale. Spring and autumn are spectacular; summer means clear passes but fire risk.

Waypoints:
1. [start | d0 | morning] Wangaratta ((-36.3576)::numeric, 146.3136::numeric) — Start in Wangaratta. Fuel up — the alpine section has limited services.
2. [scenic | d0 | morning] King Valley Wine Region ((-36.6500)::numeric, 146.5500::numeric) — The road passes through prosecco and nebbiolo vineyards. Cellar doors open 10am.
3. [food | d0 | morning] Bright ((-36.7269)::numeric, 146.9619::numeric) — Stop in Bright — the 'alpine village'. Famous for autumn foliage (April/May). Good café lunch options.
4. [pass_summit | d0 | afternoon] Falls Creek (1,780m) ((-36.8683)::numeric, 147.2761::numeric) — Ski resort perched on a plateau. In summer the ski runs are mountain bike trails. Views across the high plains.
5. [pass_summit | d0 | afternoon] Mount Hotham (1,750m) ((-36.9833)::numeric, 147.1500::numeric) — Second summit. The road between Falls Creek and Hotham is the highest in Victoria — exposed, stunning, and worth slowing down for.
6. [scenic | d0 | afternoon] Dinner Plain ((-36.9358)::numeric, 147.2011::numeric) — Historic high plains cattle station-turned-ski village. The Alpine Road begins its long descent here.
7. [end | d0 | evening] Bairnsdale ((-37.8340)::numeric, 147.6135::numeric) — End in Bairnsdale on the Gippsland plain. The descent through mountain ash is as impressive as the climb.

### Snowy Mountains Circuit — Kosciuszko High Country
Meta: AU | AU-NSW | Cooma | 1d | 220km | moderate | paved | 1900m↑ | pick:no
Start: -36.2340, 149.1269
Description: Australia's highest road rides through Kosciuszko National Park, home of the nation's highest peak (2,228m). The Alpine Way crosses from Cooma to Thredbo, passing subalpine meadows, snow gum forests, and the hydroelectric Snowy Scheme infrastructure. Open October–April (snow closes passes in winter).

Waypoints:
1. [start | d0 | morning] Cooma ((-36.2340)::numeric, 149.1269::numeric) — Start in Cooma — the 'capital of the Snowy Mountains'. Fuel up; limited on the Alpine Way.
2. [scenic | d0 | morning] Jindabyne ((-36.4174)::numeric, 148.6252::numeric) — Lake Jindabyne from the dam wall — the drowned town of Old Jindabyne lies beneath the surface. Good bakery here.
3. [scenic | d0 | morning] Perisher Valley ((-36.4050)::numeric, 148.4100::numeric) — Australia's largest ski resort. In summer: wildflower meadows and mountain bike trails.
4. [pass_summit | d0 | morning] Charlotte Pass (1,834m) ((-36.4300)::numeric, 148.3520::numeric) — Highest point accessible by road. The walk to Kosciuszko summit (3hrs return) starts here.
5. [scenic | d0 | afternoon] Thredbo Ski Village ((-36.5044)::numeric, 148.3007::numeric) — Europe-style alpine village. The Kosciuszko Express chairlift runs year-round for non-skiers.
6. [food | d0 | afternoon] Talbingo Reservoir ((-35.7000)::numeric, 148.3000::numeric) — The Blowering Reservoir area — popular for fishing and the sweeping valley views returning north.
7. [end | d0 | afternoon] Tumut ((-35.2991)::numeric, 148.2233::numeric) — End in Tumut in the Snowy Valley. Cider orchard country.

### Gibb River Road — Kimberley Wilderness
Meta: AU | AU-WA | Derby | 3d | 660km | challenging | mixed | 3200m↑ | pick:yes
Start: -17.3086, 123.6269
Description: The Gibb River Road is one of the world's great adventure rides — 660km of unsealed red laterite through the Kimberley, Australia's most remote wilderness. Dramatic gorges, Aboriginal rock art, swimming holes, million-acre cattle stations, and the Cockburn Range backdrop. Accessible only May–October (Wet season closes the road completely). A 4WD adventure bike is recommended.

Waypoints:
1. [start | d0 | morning] Derby ((-17.3086)::numeric, 123.6269::numeric) — Start in Derby. Fuel, food, and critical bike check before the Gibb. Carry 20L extra fuel minimum.
2. [scenic | d0 | morning] Windjana Gorge National Park ((-17.4100)::numeric, 124.9400::numeric) — Ancient Devonian reef limestone gorge. Freshwater crocodiles line the banks. Aboriginal Bunuba sacred site.
3. [overnight | d0 | evening] Lennard River Gorge Camp ((-16.9500)::numeric, 125.1500::numeric) — Overnight at a station campground near Lennard River. Bush camping at its finest.
4. [scenic | d1 | morning] Bell Gorge ((-16.9500)::numeric, 125.3500::numeric) — The Gibb's most famous swimming hole — a series of tiered rock pools and waterfalls in stunning burnt-orange gorge country.
5. [photo | d1 | afternoon] Barnett River Gorge ((-16.6700)::numeric, 125.8700::numeric) — Less-visited gorge reached via short walk. Often have it to yourself.
6. [overnight | d1 | evening] Mount Barnett Station ((-16.6630)::numeric, 125.9178::numeric) — Overnight at Mount Barnett Station. Real outback hospitality. Fuel available (limited).
7. [scenic | d2 | morning] Manning Gorge ((-16.6900)::numeric, 125.9400::numeric) — The gorge walk from Manning Gorge camp leads to a beautiful waterfall via a river crossing.
8. [photo | d2 | morning] Emma Gorge ((-15.8770)::numeric, 127.2010::numeric) — El Questro Station's signature gorge — a slot canyon ending in a thermal pool. 45-minute walk.
9. [fuel | d2 | afternoon] El Questro Station ((-16.0000)::numeric, 127.3300::numeric) — Last fuel and food before Kununurra. The El Questro wilderness park covers 1 million acres.
10. [end | d2 | afternoon] Kununurra ((-15.7750)::numeric, 128.7389::numeric) — End in Kununurra. Celebratory cold beer well deserved. Lake Argyle (Australia's largest man-made lake) is 70km south.

### Blue Mountains Circuit — NSW
Meta: AU | AU-NSW | Katoomba | 1d | 160km | easy | paved | 1100m↑ | pick:no
Start: -33.7127, 150.3148
Description: The Blue Mountains rise sharply from the Sydney basin, and the Great Western Highway takes you to a plateau 1,000m above sea level. Katoomba's Three Sisters and Echo Point are Australia's most visited natural attraction after the Reef. The Bells Line of Road is the superior motorcycle road — narrow, forested, and uncrowded.

Waypoints:
1. [start | d0 | morning] Katoomba ((-33.7127)::numeric, 150.3148::numeric) — Start at Echo Point. The Three Sisters sandstone formation and the vast Jamison Valley below are immediately impressive.
2. [photo | d0 | morning] Three Sisters Lookout ((-33.7299)::numeric, 150.3114::numeric) — Three sandstone pillars rising 900m above the valley. Aboriginal Dreamtime legend involves a witch-doctor and three sisters turned to stone.
3. [scenic | d0 | morning] Govetts Leap Lookout ((-33.6313)::numeric, 150.3116::numeric) — Arguably the finest Blue Mountains lookout — 180° view of the Grose Valley with 180m waterfall below.
4. [scenic | d0 | afternoon] Mount Tomah Botanic Garden ((-33.5483)::numeric, 150.4100::numeric) — Cool climate garden on the Bells Line of Road. Outstanding mountain views.
5. [food | d0 | afternoon] Bilpin Apple Orchards ((-33.4883)::numeric, 150.5217::numeric) — The Bells Line descends through orchard country. Fresh cider and apple pie at roadside stalls.
6. [end | d0 | afternoon] Jenolan Caves ((-33.8225)::numeric, 150.0239::numeric) — The world's oldest known open cave system (340 million years). The winding approach road through the Grand Arch is spectacular.
