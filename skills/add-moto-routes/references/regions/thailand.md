### Mae Hong Son Loop — Northern Thailand Highlands
Meta: TH | TH-57 | Chiang Mai | 3d | 600km | moderate | paved | 5200m↑ | pick:yes
Start: 18.7883, 98.9853
Description: The Mae Hong Son Loop is Southeast Asia's most celebrated motorcycle route — a 600km circuit through the mountains of northern Thailand. The road crosses 1,864 curves (counted), passes through hill-tribe villages, Buddhist temples, and the misty Mae Hong Son valley. Best ridden October–February (post-monsoon, cool and clear).

Waypoints:
1. [start | d0 | morning] Chiang Mai (18.7883, 98.9853) — Start in Chiang Mai. Fuel up at the city edge. Take Route 108 south toward Mae Sariang.
2. [scenic | d0 | morning] Ob Luang Gorge National Park (18.4000, 98.5500) — A narrow gorge where the Chaem River cuts through granite walls. Short walk to the suspension bridge above the gorge.
3. [food | d0 | afternoon] Mae Sariang (18.1727, 97.9340) — Lunch in Mae Sariang on the Salween River. A relaxed riverside market town. Burmese influence in the temples here.
4. [overnight | d0 | evening] Mae Hong Son (19.3022, 97.9686) — Overnight in Mae Hong Son. Small mountain city surrounded by peaks with a beautiful lake in the centre. Morning mist most mornings.
5. [photo | d1 | morning] Mae Hong Son Wat Jong Kham (19.3010, 97.9667) — The twin temples on the lake, reflected in still water at dawn. One of Thailand's most photographed temple scenes.
6. [scenic | d1 | morning] Pang Mapha Caves (19.5167, 98.2500) — The area around Pang Mapha has extensive cave systems with prehistoric coffins inside. Day detour possible.
7. [scenic | d1 | afternoon] Pai Canyon Viewpoint (19.3500, 98.4500) — Narrow red-earth canyon ridges above the Pai valley. Excellent sunset spot.
8. [overnight | d1 | evening] Pai (19.3561, 98.4382) — Overnight in Pai — the bohemian backpacker town at 800m. Heavily developed now but the mountain setting is still beautiful. Hot springs 7km east.
9. [scenic | d2 | morning] Pai River Valley (19.3500, 98.5000) — The road north from Pai is the mountain section — constant 180° bends through limestone karst and teak forest.
10. [food | d2 | morning] Mae Rim (18.9142, 98.9603) — Café stop in the orchid and elephant country north of Chiang Mai.
11. [end | d2 | afternoon] Chiang Mai (18.7883, 98.9853) — Return to Chiang Mai completing the loop. 1,864 curves total.

### Doi Inthanon — Thailand's Highest Mountain Circuit
Meta: TH | TH-50 | Chiang Mai | 1d | 180km | moderate | paved | 2200m↑ | pick:yes
Start: 18.7883, 98.9853
Description: Doi Inthanon (2,565m) is Thailand's highest peak and the road to its summit is one of the best day rides in the country. The highway winds through montane forest past waterfalls, twin pagodas, and highland Hmong and Karen villages. At the summit, temperature drops below 10°C while the lowlands bake at 35°C.

Waypoints:
1. [start | d0 | morning] Chiang Mai (18.7883, 98.9853) — Start in Chiang Mai. Head south on Route 108.
2. [scenic | d0 | morning] Chom Thong (18.4194, 98.6700) — Entry to Doi Inthanon National Park. The Doi Inthanon road branches left from the main highway.
3. [photo | d0 | morning] Wachiratharn Waterfall (18.5547, 98.5847) — Powerful 70m waterfall, most dramatic in rainy season (Aug–Oct). Good mist and rainbow conditions.
4. [pass_summit | d0 | morning] Doi Inthanon Summit — 2,565m (18.5895, 98.4868) — Thailand's highest point. Cold. Cloud forest. Royal chedis (pagodas) commissioned by the King and Queen.
5. [scenic | d0 | afternoon] Kew Mae Pan Trail (18.5700, 98.4600) — Walk above the clouds on the ridge trail. Views over Myanmar on clear days.
6. [food | d0 | afternoon] Hmong Village Market (18.5300, 98.5000) — Hill tribe market at 1,800m. Strawberries, persimmons, and embroidered hill tribe crafts.
7. [scenic | d0 | afternoon] Mae Klang Waterfall (18.5200, 98.5900) — Lower falls near the park entry — wide cascade popular with Thai picnickers.
8. [end | d0 | afternoon] Chiang Mai (18.7883, 98.9853) — Return to Chiang Mai via Chom Thong and Route 108.

### Chiang Rai Golden Triangle Circuit
Meta: TH | TH-57 | Chiang Rai | 1d | 160km | easy | paved | 1100m↑ | pick:no
Start: 19.9105, 99.8406
Description: A day loop from Chiang Rai to the Golden Triangle — where Thailand, Myanmar, and Laos meet at the Mekong River. The route passes Doi Tung Royal Project (reforestation replacing opium fields), tea plantations at Doi Mae Salong, and the hall of opium museum at Sop Ruak.

Waypoints:
1. [start | d0 | morning] Chiang Rai (19.9105, 99.8406) — Start in Chiang Rai. The White Temple (Wat Rong Khun) just south of town is one of Thailand's most extraordinary modern temples.
2. [scenic | d0 | morning] Doi Tung Royal Project (20.2967, 99.8005) — Highland gardens and reforestation project replacing the former opium fields. The Royal Villa sits in cool mountain gardens at 1,500m.
3. [scenic | d0 | morning] Mae Salong Tea Plantations (20.2667, 99.6833) — Yunnan-Chinese village at 1,300m, its hillsides terraced with tea. Oolong and green tea for sale from every shop.
4. [photo | d0 | afternoon] Golden Triangle (20.3533, 100.0867) — Where Thailand, Myanmar, and Laos converge at the Mekong. Dramatic river views. The Hall of Opium museum is first-rate.
5. [scenic | d0 | afternoon] Chiang Saen Ruins (20.2767, 100.0850) — Ancient Lanna Kingdom walled city on the Mekong bank, 14th century. Scattered ruins in a quiet park.
6. [end | d0 | afternoon] Chiang Khong (20.2817, 100.4000) — End in Chiang Khong on the Mekong. The Friendship Bridge across to Laos (Houayxai) is visible. Return to Chiang Rai via Route 1020.
