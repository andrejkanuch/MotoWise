### Milford Sound Road — Fiordland
Meta: NZ | NZ-OTA | Te Anau | 1d | 290km | moderate | paved | 1800m↑ | pick:yes
Start: -45.4133, 167.7190
Description: The road to Milford Sound is arguably the most spectacular cul-de-sac in the world. Te Anau to Milford passes through ancient beech forest, alpine valleys, and the Mirror Lakes before entering the Homer Tunnel — blasted through solid granite at 945m. Milford Sound itself rises 1,600m from the sea in a glacially carved fjord. The return journey, going the same road, looks entirely different.

Waypoints:
1. [start | d0 | morning] Te Anau ((-45.4133)::numeric, 167.7190::numeric) — Start in Te Anau on the shore of NZ's second-largest lake. Early departure recommended — Milford gets crowded by 11am.
2. [scenic | d0 | morning] Eglinton Valley ((-44.9000)::numeric, 168.0500::numeric) — The broad U-shaped glacial valley opens up. Beech forest and tussock grassland.
3. [photo | d0 | morning] Mirror Lakes ((-44.8667)::numeric, 168.0333::numeric) — Perfect reflections of the Earl Mountains in calm conditions. Best before 10am before wind.
4. [scenic | d0 | morning] The Divide — Key Summit ((-44.8000)::numeric, 168.1000::numeric) — The key watershed between east and west. A short walk gives panoramic views over three different catchments.
5. [pass_summit | d0 | morning] Homer Tunnel Entry ((-44.7478)::numeric, 168.1135::numeric) — The raw unlined tunnel blasted through the Alps in 1954. Wet, dark, and dramatic. Kea parrots (large alpine parrots) wait at the entrance.
6. [scenic | d0 | morning] Cleddau Canyon ((-44.7000)::numeric, 168.0667::numeric) — On the Milford side of Homer. The valley is suddenly deep and narrow with vertical walls.
7. [photo | d0 | afternoon] Milford Sound — Mitre Peak ((-44.6671)::numeric, 167.9255::numeric) — 1,692m Mitre Peak rises from the sea. Bowen Falls cascades 162m to the right. If raining (320 rain days/year): still spectacular — hundreds of temporary waterfalls pour over the cliffs.
8. [end | d0 | afternoon] Milford Sound ((-44.6671)::numeric, 167.9255::numeric) — Take the 1.5hr cruise before riding back. The tunnel one-way system means you may wait for the road to open returning. Return to Te Anau for overnight.

### Arthur's Pass SH73 — Trans-Alpine Highway
Meta: NZ | NZ-CAN | Christchurch | 1d | 235km | moderate | paved | 1600m↑ | pick:yes
Start: -43.5321, 172.6362
Description: State Highway 73 crosses the Southern Alps between Canterbury and the West Coast — one of New Zealand's finest cross-mountain routes. The road climbs through the Canterbury Plains, enters Castle Hill Basin (a karst landscape of enormous boulders), crests Arthur's Pass (920m), and descends through the dramatic Otira Gorge to the West Coast.

Waypoints:
1. [start | d0 | morning] Christchurch ((-43.5321)::numeric, 172.6362::numeric) — Start in Christchurch. Head west on SH73 across the Canterbury Plains.
2. [scenic | d0 | morning] Darfield & Plains Entry ((-43.4833)::numeric, 172.1167::numeric) — The plains give way to the first foothills. The Southern Alps wall appears ahead.
3. [scenic | d0 | morning] Castle Hill ((-43.2167)::numeric, 171.6667::numeric) — Limestone boulders the size of houses scattered across an alpine basin. Sacred Māori site. Used in filming The Chronicles of Narnia.
4. [scenic | d0 | morning] Craigieburn Forest ((-43.1667)::numeric, 171.7000::numeric) — Beech forest ski area. Kea Alpine Parrots are common here — the only true alpine parrot in the world.
5. [pass_summit | d0 | morning] Arthur's Pass Village (920m) ((-42.9447)::numeric, 171.5653::numeric) — Alpine village in the pass. The Arthur's Pass National Park visitor centre has good information. Kea sit on motorcycles if left unattended.
6. [scenic | d0 | afternoon] Otira Gorge ((-42.9006)::numeric, 171.5522::numeric) — The steepest section: the road drops 300m in 3km through a tight gorge. Remarkable engineering.
7. [food | d0 | afternoon] Hokitika ((-42.7167)::numeric, 170.9667::numeric) — The Jade (Pounamu) capital of New Zealand. The Hokitika Gorge (10km detour) has impossibly turquoise water.
8. [end | d0 | afternoon] Greymouth ((-42.4537)::numeric, 171.2108::numeric) — End in Greymouth, the West Coast's largest town. The TranzAlpine train runs the same route — coincides with your return if taking the scenic rail back.

### Mackenzie Basin — Mount Cook Circuit
Meta: NZ | NZ-CAN | Lake Tekapo | 1d | 280km | easy | paved | 1200m↑ | pick:yes
Start: -44.0056, 170.4783
Description: The Mackenzie Basin is New Zealand's most dramatic open landscape — tawny tussock plains between snow-capped ranges, and two of the country's most famous lakes (Tekapo and Pukaki, both glacially turquoise). Mount Cook (Aoraki, 3,724m) dominates the western skyline. The Church of the Good Shepherd at Lake Tekapo is one of the most photographed buildings in NZ.

Waypoints:
1. [start | d0 | morning] Lake Tekapo ((-44.0056)::numeric, 170.4783::numeric) — Start at Lake Tekapo. The Church of the Good Shepherd framing the turquoise lake is best at dawn. The Tekapo Springs observatory is an International Dark Sky Reserve.
2. [photo | d0 | morning] Lake Pukaki ((-44.1667)::numeric, 170.1333::numeric) — Turn off SH8 to the lake edge. The view of Aoraki/Mt Cook reflected in the turquoise lake is the classic Southern Alps image.
3. [scenic | d0 | morning] Mount Cook Road (SH80) ((-43.8500)::numeric, 170.1500::numeric) — 55km along the lake and through the Hooker Valley to the mountain. The road ends at the village with Aoraki filling the frame.
4. [pass_summit | d0 | morning] Mount Cook Village ((-43.7348)::numeric, 170.0946::numeric) — The Hooker Valley track (3hrs return) walks to glacier viewpoints. Hermitage Hotel for coffee and the huge Mt Cook view.
5. [scenic | d0 | afternoon] Lindis Pass ((-44.5792)::numeric, 169.6444::numeric) — Return via SH8 through the Lindis Pass. Soft rounded tussock hills, no guardrails, pure New Zealand pastoral.
6. [food | d0 | afternoon] Omarama ((-44.4850)::numeric, 169.9700::numeric) — Small town at the junction — the Clay Cliffs geological formation is 5km north: eroded pinnacles from an ancient lake bed.
7. [end | d0 | afternoon] Twizel ((-44.2653)::numeric, 170.1000::numeric) — End in Twizel — base town for Mackenzie activities and the Lord of the Rings filming locations nearby.

### Coromandel Peninsula Circuit
Meta: NZ | NZ-WKO | Thames | 1d | 190km | moderate | paved | 1400m↑ | pick:no
Start: -37.1414, 175.5451
Description: The Coromandel Peninsula juts north of Auckland into the Hauraki Gulf, with a rugged mountain spine dividing the west coast (sheltered) from the east (Pacific Ocean beaches and pohutukawa trees). The circuit via the scenic SH25A over the peninsula's high point offers both coasts. Hot Water Beach and Cathedral Cove are iconic.

Waypoints:
1. [start | d0 | morning] Thames ((-37.1414)::numeric, 175.5451::numeric) — Start in Thames at the peninsula's base. Fuel up for the circuit.
2. [scenic | d0 | morning] Coromandel Town ((-36.7631)::numeric, 175.4987::numeric) — Gold rush heritage town. The Driving Creek Railway (narrow gauge through kauri forest) is a quirky attraction.
3. [scenic | d0 | morning] Colville Road ((-36.5000)::numeric, 175.4667::numeric) — The northern peninsula tip — unsealed but passable in dry conditions. Moehau Range wilderness.
4. [photo | d0 | afternoon] Hahei — Cathedral Cove ((-36.8333)::numeric, 175.7667::numeric) — Walk (45 min) to the famous stone arch framing a cove. Accessible by sea kayak in summer.
5. [photo | d0 | afternoon] Hot Water Beach ((-36.8756)::numeric, 175.8017::numeric) — Dig a hot pool in the sand at low tide — geothermal water seeps through. Bizarre and wonderful.
6. [scenic | d0 | afternoon] SH25A Tapu-Coroglen Road ((-37.0500)::numeric, 175.6500::numeric) — The mountain crossing of the peninsula. One-lane in sections, with views to both coasts.
7. [end | d0 | afternoon] Thames ((-37.1414)::numeric, 175.5451::numeric) — Return to Thames completing the Coromandel circuit.
