### Manali to Leh Highway — The Himalayan Classic
Meta: IN | IN-HP | Manali | 3d | 480km | expert | mixed | 9800m↑ | pick:yes
Start: 32.2396, 77.1887
Description: The world's highest motorable road corridor, crossing five high passes above 4,000m through the barren moonscapes of Ladakh. Built by the Indian Army Border Roads Organisation and open only June–September. At 5,328m Tanglang La is the second-highest motorable pass on Earth. The landscape shifts from Kullu Valley pine forests to complete high-altitude desert within 200km. Altitude sickness is a real risk — acclimatise properly and ascend slowly.

Waypoints:
1. [start | d0 | morning] Manali (32.2396, 77.1887) — Start in Manali (2,050m). Check bike mechanicals, carry water, snacks, and altitude medication. Road opens usually mid-June.
2. [pass_summit | d0 | morning] Rohtang Pass (3,978m) (32.3728, 77.2408) — First high pass. Often the most crowded — get through early. Views back to Manali valley.
3. [scenic | d0 | afternoon] Keylong (32.5553, 77.0236) — The Lahaul Valley floor at 3,100m. The landscape has already transformed to Tibetan high desert.
4. [overnight | d0 | evening] Jispa (32.6806, 77.1547) — Overnight in Jispa (3,200m). Last comfortable accommodation before the high passes. Rustic camps and dhabas.
5. [pass_summit | d1 | morning] Baralacha La (4,892m) (32.7333, 77.3833) — First true high pass. Snow-covered ridgelines, meltwater streams crossing the road, yak herders.
6. [scenic | d1 | afternoon] Sarchu Plains (32.9500, 77.7167) — High-altitude plateau at 4,253m. Tent camps only — no permanent buildings. Dramatic gorge scenery.
7. [overnight | d1 | evening] Sarchu (32.9500, 77.7167) — Overnight at Sarchu tent camp. Altitude can cause headaches and nausea. Drink water, go slow.
8. [pass_summit | d2 | morning] Nakee La (4,739m) (33.1200, 77.7600) — Series of 21 hairpins called the Gata Loops leads to Nakee La.
9. [pass_summit | d2 | morning] Lachulung La (5,065m) (33.1833, 77.8500) — Second major pass of Day 3. Sparse oxygen, incredible Zanskar Range views.
10. [pass_summit | d2 | morning] Tanglang La (5,328m) (33.5167, 77.7167) — The highest point of the Manali-Leh road. Colourful prayer flags and a board proclaiming the altitude. One of the world's great motorcycle moments.
11. [scenic | d2 | afternoon] Upshi (33.7167, 78.0000) — The road rejoins the Indus River valley. Tibetan architecture and monasteries begin appearing.
12. [end | d2 | afternoon] Leh (34.1526, 77.5771) — End in Leh (3,500m). Ladakhi capital with Buddhist monasteries, excellent guesthouses and repair shops. Rest here before any further riding.

### Spiti Valley Circuit — Valley of the Gods
Meta: IN | IN-HP | Shimla | 3d | 450km | expert | mixed | 8500m↑ | pick:yes
Start: 31.1048, 77.1734
Description: Spiti is a cold desert valley at 3,800–4,500m in the remote Trans-Himalayan region of Himachal Pradesh. Cut off by snow for six months, it preserves an ancient Tibetan Buddhist culture alongside dramatic barren landscapes. The circuit from Shimla via Kinnaur Valley to Kaza and out through Kunzum La is one of India's most challenging and rewarding motorcycle journeys.

Waypoints:
1. [start | d0 | morning] Shimla (31.1048, 77.1734) — Start in Shimla, colonial hill station. Head east on NH5 toward Kinnaur.
2. [scenic | d0 | morning] Kinnaur Valley (31.5000, 78.0000) — The Sutlej River gorge narrows dramatically. The road is blasted into cliff faces barely wide enough for a truck.
3. [scenic | d0 | afternoon] Sangla Valley (31.4167, 78.2333) — A green side valley at 2,700m. Orchards and traditional Kinnauri wooden architecture.
4. [overnight | d0 | evening] Kaza (32.2284, 78.0734) — Overnight in Kaza (3,800m) — the 'capital' of Spiti. Well-equipped for travellers. Acclimatise here.
5. [scenic | d1 | morning] Key Monastery (32.3000, 78.0167) — Fortress-monastery on a conical hill 4,166m. One of the finest monastery settings in the Himalayas. Resident lamas.
6. [scenic | d1 | morning] Kibber Village (32.4000, 78.0000) — One of the world's highest permanent villages at 4,205m. Stone houses and prayer flags against brown desert ridges.
7. [photo | d1 | afternoon] Chandratal Lake (32.4833, 77.6167) — The 'Moon Lake' at 4,300m. A perfect teardrop lake in the high plateau — one of India's most beautiful alpine lakes.
8. [overnight | d1 | evening] Batal (32.4167, 77.5833) — Overnight at Batal — a single dhaba (roadside restaurant) at 3,960m. Extremely basic but legendary among Spiti riders.
9. [pass_summit | d2 | morning] Kunzum La (4,551m) (32.4500, 77.5833) — The gateway pass between Spiti and the Lahaul Valley. Rotate a prayer wheel for safe passage. Traditional.
10. [scenic | d2 | afternoon] Rohtang Pass (3,978m) (32.3728, 77.2408) — Cross Rohtang again heading west. Often congested with tourists in summer.
11. [end | d2 | afternoon] Manali (32.2396, 77.1887) — End in Manali, completing the Spiti loop.

### Rajasthan Desert Route — Jodhpur to Jaisalmer
Meta: IN | IN-RJ | Jodhpur | 2d | 430km | moderate | paved | 1200m↑ | pick:yes
Start: 26.2389, 73.0243
Description: Two days riding through the Thar Desert from the Blue City to the Golden City — two of Rajasthan's most iconic royal fortresses. The NH125 crosses flat semi-desert scrubland with camel caravans, peacocks on roadside trees, and the occasional village where time has barely moved. Jaisalmer fort rises from the desert like a mirage.

Waypoints:
1. [start | d0 | morning] Jodhpur (26.2389, 73.0243) — Start at Mehrangarh Fort — the most dramatic citadel in Rajasthan. Blue-painted old city below. Fuel up at the edge of town heading west.
2. [scenic | d0 | morning] Osian Temple Complex (26.7167, 72.8833) — 8th–11th century Hindu and Jain temples rising from the desert sand. Almost no tourists. Extraordinary.
3. [food | d0 | afternoon] Pokhran (26.9167, 71.9167) — Junction town in the Thar. India's nuclear tests were conducted in this desert. Dal baati churma (Rajasthani dish) for lunch.
4. [overnight | d0 | evening] Jaisalmer (26.9157, 70.9083) — Overnight in Jaisalmer — the Golden City. The sandstone fort is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and a living fort with 3,000 residents inside its walls.
5. [photo | d1 | morning] Jaisalmer Fort (26.9125, 70.9056) — Dawn light turns the sandstone gold. Walk the ramparts overlooking the desert. The best views are from Vyas Chhatri cenotaphs.
6. [scenic | d1 | morning] Sam Sand Dunes (26.8741, 70.5780) — The Thar Desert's classic dunes, 45km west of Jaisalmer. Camel rides at sunrise before the tourist crowd. The dunes are extensive and genuinely beautiful.
7. [scenic | d1 | afternoon] Tanot Mata Temple (27.0833, 70.2667) — Remote border temple near the Pakistan border. Protected during the 1971 war — shells that hit the temple failed to explode. Sacred site.
8. [end | d1 | afternoon] Jodhpur (26.2389, 73.0243) — Return to Jodhpur via NH125 for the full desert loop.

### Western Ghats — Coorg Coffee Hills
Meta: IN | IN-KA | Mysuru | 1d | 200km | moderate | paved | 2100m↑ | pick:no
Start: 12.2958, 76.6394
Description: The Western Ghats between Mysore and the Malabar Coast offer lush tropical riding entirely unlike the Himalayas — coffee estates, cardamom forests, waterfalls, and elephant country. The road climbs through Kodagu (Coorg), India's Scotland, to the highest peak in Karnataka at Tadiandamol.

Waypoints:
1. [start | d0 | morning] Mysuru (12.2958, 76.6394) — Start in Mysore. The city's Maharaja's Palace is unmissable. Head southwest into the Ghats on NH275.
2. [scenic | d0 | morning] Nagarhole National Park (11.9667, 76.1333) — Tiger reserve and elephant corridor. Dawn wildlife drive possible before riding. Road through the park is excellent.
3. [scenic | d0 | morning] Coorg Coffee Estates (12.4167, 75.7333) — The road enters Kodagu district. Coffee and cardamom plantations fill the valleys. Air smells incredible.
4. [photo | d0 | afternoon] Abbey Falls (12.4197, 75.7333) — 70m waterfall near Madikeri. Short walk from the road through coffee estate.
5. [food | d0 | afternoon] Madikeri (12.4194, 75.7382) — The hill station capital of Coorg. Lunch at a local Coorgi restaurant. Try pandi curry (pork).
6. [scenic | d0 | afternoon] Talacauvery (12.3833, 75.4833) — The source of the Cauvery River — a sacred pool at 1,276m. Excellent viewpoint.
7. [end | d0 | afternoon] Virajpet (12.1956, 75.8050) — End in Virajpet, a relaxed coffee-trading town.
