### Carretera Austral — Chile's Southern Highway
Meta: CL | CL-AI | Puerto Montt | 3d | 620km | challenging | mixed | 5800m↑ | pick:yes
Start: -41.4693, -72.9424
Description: Chile's Carretera Austral (Route 7) is one of the world's greatest adventure motorcycle roads — 1,200km of mostly unpaved highway through Chilean Patagonia, built by Pinochet's military in the 1970s. This 3-day highlights run covers the northern section from Puerto Montt to Coyhaique, passing fjords, hanging glaciers, ancient Alerce forests, and the permanently erupting Chaitén Volcano.

Waypoints:
1. [start | d0 | morning] Puerto Montt ((-41.4693)::numeric, (-72.9424)::numeric) — Start in Puerto Montt. Take the Caleta La Arena–Caleta Puelche ferry (30 min) to begin the Carretera. Check ferry schedule the night before.
2. [ferry | d0 | morning] Caleta La Arena Ferry Crossing ((-41.5500)::numeric, (-72.6500)::numeric) — Short ferry across the Reloncaví estuary. Required to access the Carretera Austral.
3. [scenic | d0 | afternoon] Cochamo Valley ((-41.4833)::numeric, (-72.3000)::numeric) — Granite walls rising 2,000m from the valley floor. Chile's Yosemite. Gravel track leads to world-class climbing.
4. [overnight | d0 | evening] Chaitén ((-42.9167)::numeric, (-72.7167)::numeric) — Overnight in Chaitén, rebuilt after the 2008 volcano eruption buried much of the town under ash. The volcano still smokes.
5. [photo | d1 | morning] Lago Yelcho ((-43.4333)::numeric, (-72.3167)::numeric) — The road follows this stunning turquoise lake through dense Valdivian rainforest.
6. [scenic | d1 | morning] La Junta ((-43.9667)::numeric, (-72.4167)::numeric) — Carved from the forest in the 1980s. Small but perfectly serviceable for fuel and lunch.
7. [photo | d1 | afternoon] Puyuhuapi Hanging Glacier ((-44.3333)::numeric, (-72.5833)::numeric) — The Queulat Hanging Glacier overhangs a cliff face in a fjord — one of the most dramatic sights on the Carretera.
8. [overnight | d1 | evening] Puyuhuapi ((-44.3333)::numeric, (-72.5833)::numeric) — Overnight in Puyuhuapi. German-founded village on the fjord. Hot springs at Termas de Puyuhuapi across the water.
9. [scenic | d2 | morning] Cisnes Valley ((-44.7500)::numeric, (-72.4000)::numeric) — The road climbs to the high pampa — open steppes after days in rainforest. The contrast is stark.
10. [fuel | d2 | afternoon] Villa Mañihuales ((-45.1667)::numeric, (-72.3167)::numeric) — Fuel here. Last reliable station before Coyhaique.
11. [end | d2 | afternoon] Coyhaique ((-45.5712)::numeric, (-72.0680)::numeric) — End in Coyhaique, the largest city on the Carretera. Well-equipped for repairs and rest. Excellent lamb asado.

### Patagonia Ruta 40 — Bariloche to El Calafate
Meta: AR | AR-Z | Bariloche | 3d | 680km | challenging | mixed | 3200m↑ | pick:yes
Start: -41.1335, -71.3103
Description: The southern section of Argentina's mythical Ruta Nacional 40 crosses the Patagonian steppe from the lake district to the glaciers. Fierce winds blow constantly from the west, paved sections alternate with gravel, and the only company for hundreds of kilometres is guanacos and condors. The reward is Perito Moreno Glacier — the world's only advancing glacier.

Waypoints:
1. [start | d0 | morning] Bariloche ((-41.1335)::numeric, (-71.3103)::numeric) — Start in Bariloche — Patagonia's ski city on the shore of Lago Nahuel Huapi. One of Argentina's most beautiful settings.
2. [scenic | d0 | morning] El Bolsón ((-41.9647)::numeric, (-71.5311)::numeric) — The hippie arts village of Patagonia. Cherry orchards, craft beer, artisan markets. A gentle start before the steppe.
3. [scenic | d0 | afternoon] Esquel ((-42.9044)::numeric, (-71.3181)::numeric) — Last city before the steppe. Old Patagonia Express steam train (La Trochita) still runs from here. Fuel up.
4. [overnight | d0 | evening] Río Mayo ((-45.6856)::numeric, (-70.2544)::numeric) — Overnight in Río Mayo — a true Patagonian estancia town. Population 3,000. One hotel, one restaurant, one street.
5. [scenic | d1 | morning] Ruta 40 Open Steppe ((-46.5000)::numeric, (-70.5000)::numeric) — Hours of nothing but horizons, guanacos, and wind. This is why people ride the Ruta 40.
6. [photo | d1 | afternoon] Cueva de las Manos ((-47.1500)::numeric, (-70.6667)::numeric) — UNESCO site — a canyon of 9,000-year-old hand-print cave paintings by the region's earliest inhabitants. Mind-bending.
7. [overnight | d1 | evening] Perito Moreno Town ((-46.5022)::numeric, (-70.9278)::numeric) — Overnight in Perito Moreno town. Not to be confused with the glacier (same name, different place). Basic but warm.
8. [scenic | d2 | morning] Los Antiguos ((-46.5500)::numeric, (-71.6167)::numeric) — On the Chilean border, Lago Buenos Aires backdrop. Cherry capital of Patagonia. The road improves here.
9. [photo | d2 | afternoon] Perito Moreno Glacier ((-50.4956)::numeric, (-73.0390)::numeric) — The climax: the 5km-wide, 60m-tall advancing glacier of Parque Nacional Los Glaciares. Continuous ice calving. Book day before.
10. [end | d2 | afternoon] El Calafate ((-50.3386)::numeric, (-72.2653)::numeric) — End in El Calafate on Lago Argentino. Tourist infrastructure for the glaciers. Well-deserved Patagonian lamb.

### Yungas Death Road — La Paz to Coroico
Meta: BO | BO-L | La Paz | 1d | 65km | challenging | mixed | 3600m↑ | pick:yes
Start: -16.4897, -68.1193
Description: The North Yungas Road descends 3,600m from the Andean altiplano at 4,650m to the subtropical Yungas cloud forest at 1,200m in just 64km. Known as the World's Most Dangerous Road, it claimed 200–300 lives annually before a bypass was built. Now primarily used by downhill mountain bikers, motorcyclists ride the return climb — steep, single-track in sections, with sheer drops and no guardrails.

Waypoints:
1. [start | d0 | morning] La Paz ((-16.4897)::numeric, (-68.1193)::numeric) — Start in La Paz (3,640m). The Bolivian capital is the world's highest administrative capital. Acclimatise at least 1 day before.
2. [pass_summit | d0 | morning] Cumbre Pass (4,650m) ((-16.4167)::numeric, (-68.0167)::numeric) — The high point. Snow and ice in mornings. Panoramic views back to Illimani volcano.
3. [scenic | d0 | morning] Unduavi Junction ((-16.4500)::numeric, (-67.8833)::numeric) — Where the new paved road and old Death Road diverge. Take the old road for the full experience.
4. [photo | d0 | afternoon] La Cumbre — Cloud Forest Entry ((-16.3833)::numeric, (-67.8500)::numeric) — The road enters cloud forest — waterfalls pour across the track, visibility drops. Temperature rises 15°C from the top.
5. [scenic | d0 | afternoon] Yolosa ((-16.2667)::numeric, (-67.7833)::numeric) — The valley floor at 1,200m. The climate is now tropical — bananas, coca plants, orchids.
6. [end | d0 | afternoon] Coroico ((-16.2000)::numeric, (-67.7333)::numeric) — End in Coroico, a small town overlooking the Yungas Valley. A cerveza fría at 1,800m looking back up at where you came from is one of motorcycling's great finishes.

### Lake District Andean Circuit — Bariloche Loop
Meta: AR | AR-RN | Bariloche | 2d | 420km | moderate | paved | 2800m↑ | pick:no
Start: -41.1335, -71.3103
Description: A 2-day circuit through Argentina's Andean lake district, skirting enormous glacial lakes under snow-capped volcanoes. The Seven Lakes Road is one of South America's most scenic drives. The border crossing into Chile at Paso Cardenal Samoré offers a short detour to Lago Puyehue in Chilean lake district.

Waypoints:
1. [start | d0 | morning] Bariloche ((-41.1335)::numeric, (-71.3103)::numeric) — Start in Bariloche. The Ruta de los Siete Lagos (Seven Lakes Road) heads north on RN231.
2. [scenic | d0 | morning] Villa La Angostura ((-40.7628)::numeric, (-71.6394)::numeric) — At the northern shore of Lago Nahuel Huapi. The Arrayanes Forest National Park here has ancient myrtle trees.
3. [scenic | d0 | afternoon] Seven Lakes Road — Lagos Correntoso, Espejo, Villarino ((-40.5000)::numeric, (-71.6000)::numeric) — The road crosses between seven lakes over 110km. Each lake a different shade of blue-green.
4. [overnight | d0 | evening] San Martín de los Andes ((-40.1577)::numeric, (-71.3531)::numeric) — Overnight in San Martín — a Swiss-style mountain village on Lago Lácar. Excellent chocolate shops and craft beer.
5. [scenic | d1 | morning] Lanín Volcano ((-39.6333)::numeric, (-71.5000)::numeric) — The perfect snow-capped cone of Lanín (3,776m) dominates the skyline. The road circles the volcano's flanks.
6. [scenic | d1 | morning] Paso Cardenal Samoré — Chile Border ((-40.5667)::numeric, (-71.9833)::numeric) — Optional short detour into Chile. The border crossing is easy and the Puyehue area has excellent hot springs.
7. [food | d1 | afternoon] Junín de los Andes ((-39.9456)::numeric, (-71.0730)::numeric) — Trout fishing capital of Patagonia. Lunch at a riverside restaurant with fresh Patagonian trout.
8. [end | d1 | afternoon] Bariloche ((-41.1335)::numeric, (-71.3103)::numeric) — Return to Bariloche via RN234. The lake views on the approach are spectacular.
