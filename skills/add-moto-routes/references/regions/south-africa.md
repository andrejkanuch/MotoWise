### Garden Route — Cape to Tsitsikamma
Meta: ZA | ZA-WC | Cape Town | 2d | 580km | moderate | paved | 3200m↑ | pick:yes
Start: -33.9249, 18.4241
Description: South Africa's most popular coastal road stretches from Cape Town through the Cape Overberg and Garden Route National Park to the Tsitsikamma forest. Day 1 crosses the dramatic mountain passes of the Cape Fold Mountains. Day 2 rides the lush 'Garden Route' proper — indigenous forest, lagoons, and the Storms River gorge.

Waypoints:
1. [start | d0 | morning] Cape Town ((-33.9249)::numeric, 18.4241::numeric) — Start in Cape Town under Table Mountain. Head east on the N2.
2. [scenic | d0 | morning] Sir Lowry's Pass ((-34.1333)::numeric, 18.9667::numeric) — The first dramatic Cape Fold Mountain pass. Views back over False Bay and the Cape Peninsula.
3. [photo | d0 | morning] Hermanus ((-34.4187)::numeric, 19.2345::numeric) — The world's best land-based whale watching (July–December). Southern Right Whales calve in Walker Bay. The cliff path follows the blows.
4. [scenic | d0 | afternoon] Hemel-en-Aarde Valley ((-34.3600)::numeric, 19.2000::numeric) — Pinot Noir country in a mountain valley. Several excellent wineries open for tastings.
5. [overnight | d0 | evening] George ((-33.9638)::numeric, 22.4656::numeric) — Overnight in George. Good facilities. Outeniqua Pass access nearby.
6. [scenic | d1 | morning] Wilderness ((-33.9908)::numeric, 22.5874::numeric) — The first Garden Route 'proper' town. Lagoon systems and beaches.
7. [photo | d1 | morning] Knysna Heads ((-34.0500)::numeric, 23.0833::numeric) — The narrow rocky entrance to Knysna Lagoon. Views from the east head are spectacular. The oysters here are famous.
8. [scenic | d1 | afternoon] Plettenberg Bay ((-34.0533)::numeric, 23.3714::numeric) — The glamorous Garden Route beach resort. Whale and dolphin watching from the beach.
9. [scenic | d1 | afternoon] Bloukrans Bridge Bungee ((-33.9667)::numeric, 23.6333::numeric) — The world's highest commercial bungee at 216m. Whether jumping or not, the bridge view over the forested gorge is dramatic.
10. [end | d1 | afternoon] Storms River ((-33.9817)::numeric, 23.8921::numeric) — End at Storms River in Tsitsikamma National Park. The suspension bridge over the river gorge is a short walk. Ancient yellowwood forest.

### Cape Peninsula Day Ride
Meta: ZA | ZA-WC | Cape Town | 1d | 150km | moderate | paved | 1400m↑ | pick:yes
Start: -33.9249, 18.4241
Description: The Cape Peninsula circuit is one of the finest day rides in Africa — from the Atlantic seaboard through Hout Bay, over Chapman's Peak, to Cape Point at the peninsula's tip, then back through the False Bay coast and Simon's Town penguin colony. The sea cliffs, whale sightings, and fynbos (Cape flora) are extraordinary.

Waypoints:
1. [start | d0 | morning] Cape Town Waterfront ((-33.9049)::numeric, 18.4186::numeric) — Start at the V&A Waterfront. Head south on the Atlantic Seaboard.
2. [scenic | d0 | morning] Chapman's Peak Drive ((-34.0833)::numeric, 18.3500::numeric) — 9km of cliff-blasted road on the Atlantic face. 114 curves. One of the world's most photographed coastal roads.
3. [food | d0 | morning] Hout Bay Harbour ((-34.0508)::numeric, 18.3588::numeric) — Fresh fish and chips at the harbour. Cape fur seals beg at the market stalls.
4. [pass_summit | d0 | afternoon] Cape Point ((-34.3572)::numeric, 18.4908::numeric) — The peninsula's tip. Portuguese explorer Bartolomeu Dias named it in 1488. Old lighthouse, flying cloud, and sheer 200m cliffs.
5. [scenic | d0 | afternoon] Cape of Good Hope ((-34.3578)::numeric, 18.4741::numeric) — The mythological 'southern tip' of Africa (it's actually Cape Agulhas). Still a dramatic headland. Baboons at the car park — secure your pannier bags.
6. [photo | d0 | afternoon] Boulders Beach Penguins ((-34.1972)::numeric, 18.4519::numeric) — A colony of 3,000 African Penguins on a suburban beach. Surreal.
7. [scenic | d0 | afternoon] False Bay Coastal Road ((-34.1500)::numeric, 18.7000::numeric) — The False Bay coast runs beneath the Hottentots Holland mountains. Southern Right Whale sightings July–October.
8. [end | d0 | evening] Cape Town ((-33.9249)::numeric, 18.4241::numeric) — Return to Cape Town over Sir Lowry's Pass or via the coastal N2.

### Sani Pass — Roof of KwaZulu-Natal
Meta: ZA | ZA-KZN | Underberg | 1d | 130km | challenging | mixed | 1900m↑ | pick:yes
Start: -29.7833, 29.5000
Description: The Sani Pass is the only road crossing between South Africa and the mountain kingdom of Lesotho in the Drakensberg region. The South African side is paved; the Lesotho side is a dramatic, rocky 4WD track climbing 1,300m in 9km. The pass summit sits at 2,874m on the Lesotho plateau. Technically demanding but unmissable.

Waypoints:
1. [start | d0 | morning] Underberg ((-29.7833)::numeric, 29.5000::numeric) — Start in Underberg. Fuel up and check permit requirements for entering Lesotho.
2. [scenic | d0 | morning] Drakensberg Foothills ((-29.7000)::numeric, 29.3500::numeric) — The road follows the Mkhomazana River valley into the Drakensberg escarpment. Grassland and protea.
3. [photo | d0 | morning] Sani Pass South Africa Gate ((-29.5900)::numeric, 29.2833::numeric) — The paved road ends at the SA border post. The real climb begins here.
4. [pass_summit | d0 | morning] Sani Pass Summit — Lesotho Border (2,874m) ((-29.5883)::numeric, 29.2750::numeric) — The highest pass in South Africa. On the Lesotho plateau, the landscape is treeless grassland at altitude. Cold.
5. [scenic | d0 | morning] Sani Mountain Lodge ((-29.5778)::numeric, 29.2700::numeric) — The highest pub in Africa at 2,874m, just across the Lesotho border. Warm Basotho blanket and brandy.
6. [scenic | d0 | afternoon] Lesotho Plateau Viewpoint ((-29.5600)::numeric, 29.3200::numeric) — The vast Lesotho plateau stretches to the horizon at 3,000m+ — the 'Kingdom in the Sky'. Horsemen in Basotho blankets.
7. [end | d0 | afternoon] Underberg ((-29.7833)::numeric, 29.5000::numeric) — Return to Underberg descending the same route. The descent shows different angles of the Drakensberg wall.

### Panorama Route — Blyde River Canyon
Meta: ZA | ZA-MP | Graskop | 1d | 180km | easy | paved | 1200m↑ | pick:no
Start: -24.9333, 30.8333
Description: The Panorama Route in Mpumalanga follows the escarpment edge above one of the world's largest canyons — the Blyde River Canyon (26km long, 800m deep). God's Window lookout, Bourke's Luck Potholes, and Three Rondavels viewpoints are the highlights. Often combined with a safari in Kruger National Park.

Waypoints:
1. [start | d0 | morning] Graskop ((-24.9333)::numeric, 30.8333::numeric) — Start in Graskop. Famous for pancakes at Harrie's. Head north on the R532 along the escarpment.
2. [photo | d0 | morning] God's Window ((-24.8667)::numeric, 30.8833::numeric) — A slot in the forest-topped escarpment opening to a 700m vertical drop. Clouds often fill the canyon below. One of Africa's great viewpoints.
3. [scenic | d0 | morning] Lisbon Falls ((-24.8667)::numeric, 30.8167::numeric) — 94m waterfall, one of South Africa's tallest. Walk through forest from the R532.
4. [photo | d0 | afternoon] Bourke's Luck Potholes ((-24.6500)::numeric, 30.8167::numeric) — Natural geological potholes sculpted by the swirling Blyde River at its confluence with the Treur River. Strange and beautiful.
5. [photo | d0 | afternoon] Three Rondavels Viewpoint ((-24.5833)::numeric, 30.7833::numeric) — Three cylindrical rock towers (rondavels) rising from the canyon floor. The canyon stretches below to an enormous reservoir.
6. [end | d0 | afternoon] Hoedspruit ((-24.3500)::numeric, 30.9500::numeric) — End in Hoedspruit near Kruger National Park's Orpen Gate.
