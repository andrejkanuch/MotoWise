## SECTION 2: MASTER EFFECTS INVENTORY

1. SPEED RAMPING (used 3x) — Shots 1 (deceleration), 3 (acceleration), 6 (gentle acceleration). Primary kinetic tool; shapes the hook, the peak, and the release.
2. SLOW-MOTION (used 2x) — Shots 2 (~40%), 4 (~50%). Emphasis on the receipt detail and the deliberate phone draw.
3. WHIP PAN TRANSITION (used 3x) — Shot 2 exit, Shot 3 internal x2. Connects the spending triptych without cuts; the engine of the signature effect.
4. WHIP-PAN TRIPTYCH / REPETITION MONTAGE (used 1x) — Shot 3. The signature effect: one gesture, three locations, accelerating.
5. CAMERA SHAKE (used 1x) — Shot 3. Builds anxiety across the triptych only; absent everywhere else.
6. RACK FOCUS (used 1x) — Shot 2. Clean optical effect, glove → face.
7. DIGITAL ZOOM (used 3x) — Shot 1 (push-in), 5 (scale-in + tap bounce), 6 (scale-out). Shot 6 pull-back mirrors and resolves Shot 1's push-in.
8. SCREEN COMPOSITE / OVERLAY (used 2x, post only) — Shots 4, 6. Real screenshot + end card in CapCut; never generated in Kling.

## SECTION 4: ENERGY ARC

- **Act 1 — Trigger (0–4s):** Personal and close. A face, a click-off, a crumpled receipt. The viewer recognizes the feeling before any product appears.
- **Act 2 — Recognition (4–7s):** The signature triptych makes the invisible visible: the same gesture bleeding money at three places. Maximum density, maximum anxiety.
- **Act 3 — Action & Resolution (7–13s):** Effects withdraw. One calm tap replaces the chaos. The frame opens up, the rider rides off in command. Stillness is the payoff — watching to the end is rewarded with the release.
