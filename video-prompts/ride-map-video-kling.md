# MotoVault — "Every Road, Remembered" (Ride Map / Where You Rode)

Core idea: you remember the corners — MotoVault remembers the roads. The hero moment is a match cut: a top-down aerial of the bike on a winding road dissolves into the same shape as a route line on the dark MotoVault map. The road literally becomes the data.
