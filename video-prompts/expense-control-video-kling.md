# MotoVault — "In Command of the Cost" (Expense Tracking · Control/Pride angle)
**Kling VIDEO 3.0 Omni · TikTok vertical 9:16 · 12s final cut · VO + captions (mute-watchable)**

Companion to "Where Does It All Go?" — same feature, opposite emotion. That video sells **relief from pain** (money vanishing → one tap fixes it). This one sells **the reward of mastery**: the rider already logs everything, and the payoff is the calm pride of knowing exactly what the machine costs. No anxiety beat. Closer in tone to the ride-map video.

**12s conform (CapCut):** generate Kling shots at full length, then trim Shot 2 to 1.5s if it overshoots. Final timeline: S1 0:00–2.0 · S2 2.0–4.0 · S3 4.0–5.5 · S4 5.5–8.5 · S5 8.5–10.0 · S6 10.0–12.0.

Story format: **ritual → log → reveal → command**
Ritual: rider back in the garage after a ride, calm, deliberate. Log: one confident tap records the fuel/service. Reveal (hero): the complete monthly cost picture assembles itself — every euro accounted for. Command: a knowing nod, rides off owning every detail.
