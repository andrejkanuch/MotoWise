## Description (for the launch page)

MotoVault is the first all-in-one AI-powered platform for motorcycle riders — combining photo-based diagnostics, structured learning, smart maintenance tracking, and expense management.

**Snap. Diagnose. Learn. Ride.**

Photograph a problem on your bike and get an instant AI diagnosis tailored to your experience level. Whether you're a beginner who needs plain-English explanations or a seasoned wrench-turner wanting technical specs, MotoVault adapts to you.

### What makes MotoVault different:

**AI Photo Diagnostics** — Take a photo of any motorcycle issue. Our Claude-powered AI analyzes it and provides a personalized diagnosis based on your bike's specs, mileage, your experience level, and even how urgent the situation is (stranded roadside vs. weekend project).

**Structured Learning** — 50+ lessons organized by difficulty, from basic maintenance to advanced repairs. Quizzes track your progress so you actually retain what you learn.

**Smart Maintenance** — Auto-populated OEM maintenance schedules for your specific bike. Mileage-based and date-based reminders so nothing falls through the cracks. A health score tells you at a glance how your bike is doing.

**Expense Tracking** — Log parts, labor, fuel, and gear costs per bike. Year-to-date spending summaries help you budget and understand true cost of ownership.

**Multi-Bike Garage** — Manage your entire fleet. Each bike gets its own maintenance history, health score, and expense records.

### Why we built this:

Every motorcycle rider knows the anxiety: "Is that noise normal? Should I take it to a mechanic? Am I overpaying for this repair?" We built MotoVault to turn motorcycle ownership from a source of worry into a confident, informed routine.

### Pricing:
- **Free:** 1 bike, basic diagnostics, 3 articles/week
- **Pro ($9.99/mo or $59.99/yr):** Unlimited bikes, full article library, smart maintenance reminders, advanced diagnostics, expense tracking

Hey Product Hunt! 👋

MotoVault brings it all together with AI at the core. The feature I'm most proud of is the photo diagnostics — you can literally photograph a problem on your bike and get a diagnosis that's aware of your specific motorcycle, your experience level, and how urgently you need help.
