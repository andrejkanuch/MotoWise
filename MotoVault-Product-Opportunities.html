1. Current Feature Audit

After a deep review of the codebase — 46 database migrations, 20 NestJS modules, 24 GraphQL mutations, 30+ queries, and the full mobile + web surface — here is what MotoVault ships today:

Garage / Bike Management

Multi-bike CRUD, NHTSA make/model lookup, mileage tracking, photo upload, soft-delete, primary bike selection.

AI Diagnostics (v2)

Step-by-step wizard (symptoms → location → timing → urgency → photo). GPT-4 Vision analysis with severity, confidence, parts/tools, next steps. Manual bike info for non-garage bikes.

Maintenance Tracking

User-created + OEM-seeded tasks. Status workflow, recurring intervals, cost/parts tracking, photo attachments, auto-create next occurrence.

Expense Tracking

Fuel, maintenance, parts, gear categories. Per-bike, per-year queries. Auto-creation from completed maintenance tasks.

Learning Hub

50+ AI-generated articles with full-text search. Quizzes per article, progress tracking, difficulty levels (beginner → advanced), best-score retention.

OEM Schedules

Factory maintenance data with 3-level fallback (model+year → make → generic). Auto-populates tasks when a bike is added.

Public Share Links

Expiring tokens (30-day default) to share bike maintenance history and expenses with buyers, mechanics, or friends — no auth required.

Subscription & Monetization

Free/Pro tiers via RevenueCat. Rate-limited AI (3/month free, unlimited pro). Global AI budget with circuit breaker.

What's Working Well

The vertical integration is strong: NHTSA data feeds bike creation → OEM schedules auto-populate tasks → completed tasks auto-log expenses → share links bundle it all for resale or mechanic visits. The AI diagnostic wizard is differentiated — no competitor offers photo-based diagnosis with structured severity/confidence output tailored to rider experience level. The learning hub creates ongoing engagement beyond maintenance events.

What's Missing

The app is currently a single-rider utility tool. There is no social layer, no ride tracking, no marketplace, no real-time data integration (OBD-II/Bluetooth), no resale valuation, and no community. The expense tracking lacks visualization and insights. The learning content is AI-generated but not curated or community-validated. The diagnostic AI has no feedback loop to improve over time.

2. Market & Competitive Landscape

Rider Demographics (2025–2026)

The U.S. motorcycle market sold 486,468 units in 2025 (down 7.6% YoY). The median rider age is ~50, with under-35 riders making up less than 25%. Over 75% ride recreationally. More than half change their own oil, and 70% reference the owner's manual for maintenance guidance. This is an audience that values self-reliance, cares about their bike's health, and is increasingly digital-savvy but underserved by fragmented tools.

Competitor Map

App Primary Focus MotoVault Advantage
MotorManage Service history, parts tracking, mileage reminders. Offline-first. 600+ brands. No AI diagnostics, no learning content, no OEM schedule auto-population
REVER Ride GPS tracking, route planning, social community, safety features (live location sharing) No maintenance/expense tracking, no diagnostics, pure ride-focused
Calimoto Curvy-road route optimization, lean angle analytics, navigation No garage management, no learning, no diagnostics
Simply Auto / Drivvo Generic vehicle maintenance & fuel logging (car-first, moto-second) Not motorcycle-specific, no OEM data, no AI, no learning

OEM Apps (BMW, HD) Connected bike data, brand-locked ecosystem Single-brand only. MotoVault is brand-agnostic and AI-powered.

Key insight: No single competitor combines maintenance + diagnostics + learning + expense tracking in one motorcycle-specific app. The market is fragmented into single-purpose tools. MotoVault's integrated approach is its moat — the opportunity is to widen that moat by adding the features riders currently piece together from 3–4 different apps.

4. Top 8 Product Opportunities

Ranked by a combination of revenue potential, retention impact, competitive moat, and effort-to-value ratio. Each opportunity includes user stories, requirements, and how it integrates with existing MotoVault infrastructure.

1
Expense Intelligence Dashboard
Revenue: Pro Upsell High Retention Effort: Low

Problem: You already capture expenses (fuel, parts, gear, maintenance) per bike per year — but users see a flat list. There are no charts, no cost-per-mile, no month-over-month trends, no category breakdowns. The data is rich; the presentation is poor. This is the lowest-hanging fruit in the entire product.

User Stories

As a multi-bike owner, I want to see total cost of ownership per bike so that I can decide which bike is worth keeping.
As a budget-conscious rider, I want to see my cost-per-mile and monthly spend trends so that I can plan my riding budget.
As a seller preparing to list a bike, I want a clean expense summary PDF I can share with buyers to prove the bike's maintenance history and total investment.

Requirements

PriorityRequirement
P0Visual expense dashboard: pie chart (by category), bar chart (monthly trend), total spend YTD, cost-per-mile calculation
P0Multi-bike comparison view: side-by-side cost of ownership
P1Exportable expense report (PDF) for resale or insurance
P1Smart alerts: "You're spending 40% more on fuel this quarter" or "This bike costs $0.32/mile vs $0.18 for your other one"
P2AI-powered spending forecast: predict annual costs based on riding patterns and maintenance schedule
Target: 60% of active users view dashboard within 30 days
Pro conversion: +8% from PDF export gating

Why now: This requires no new data — just a new UI layer over existing expenses and maintenance_tasks tables. You already have motorcycle.mileage_km for cost-per-mile. Ship in 2–3 weeks.

2
Resale Value Tracker & Bike Passport
Revenue: Premium Feature Competitive Moat Effort: Medium

Problem: KBB and NADAguides give generic resale estimates disconnected from actual maintenance records. A bike with a perfect documented service history is worth 15–25% more than one without — but there's no tool that links the two. MotoVault already has maintenance history + expenses + share links. The "Bike Passport" is one integration away from being a killer feature for private sales.

User Stories

As a rider considering selling, I want to see my bike's estimated resale value alongside its documented service history so that I can price it competitively.
As a used bike buyer, I want to scan a QR code on a listing and see a verified maintenance history so that I can trust what I'm buying.
As a collector or enthusiast, I want to track my bike's value appreciation over time so that I know when to sell or hold.

Requirements

PriorityRequirement
P0Bike Passport: branded, shareable page (enhance existing share link) showing full maintenance timeline, expenses, mileage, photos, and OEM schedule compliance
P0QR code generation per bike — scannable in-person at dealerships or private sales
P1Resale value estimate integration (KBB/NADA API or web scraping) with "MotoVault Verified" badge for bikes with >80% OEM schedule compliance
P1Value trend chart: estimated value over time based on mileage, age, and market data
P2"Best time to sell" AI recommendation based on seasonal demand and depreciation curves
Target: 30% of users generate a Bike Passport within 90 days
Share rate: 15% of Passports shared externally

Why this matters: This transforms MotoVault from a "maintenance tracker" into a "bike ownership platform." The Bike Passport becomes the reason sellers stay on the app for years — every logged expense increases their bike's provable value. It's a self-reinforcing retention loop.

3
Diagnostic AI v3: Audio Analysis + Community Feedback Loop
Competitive Moat High Retention Effort: Medium

Problem: Your diagnostic v2 is the strongest feature in the app, but it has two blind spots. First, riders describe problems by sound ("clicking when I turn," "grinding under braking") — the AI interprets text descriptions, but audio clips would dramatically improve accuracy. Second, there's no feedback mechanism. When the AI says "likely a worn chain tensioner," there's no way for the user to confirm or deny after fixing the issue. Without that loop, the product never gets smarter.

User Stories

As a rider hearing an unusual sound, I want to record a 10-second audio clip so the AI can analyze the frequency and pattern to narrow down the issue.
As a rider who fixed the issue, I want to mark what the actual problem was so that MotoVault learns from real-world outcomes.
As a beginner rider, I want to see "Similar diagnoses on this make/model" so I know I'm not alone and can learn from others' fixes.

Requirements

PriorityRequirement
P0Audio recording in diagnostic wizard (10s max, compressed upload to Supabase Storage)
P0Post-diagnosis feedback: "Was this helpful? What was the actual issue?" — stored as diagnostic outcome
P1Audio spectrogram analysis via Whisper + GPT-4 Audio (describe the sound, correlate with known patterns)
P1"Common issues for [Make] [Model] [Year]" section — aggregated from anonymized diagnostic data
P2Mechanic verification badge: let shops confirm diagnoses to build a ground-truth dataset
Target: 25% of diagnostics include audio within 60 days
Feedback rate: 40% of users submit outcome data
4
Ride Logging with Safety Contact
High Retention Revenue: Pro Feature Effort: High

Problem: REVER has millions of downloads for ride tracking alone. Riders want to log their rides, track distance/duration, and — critically — share live location with a safety contact. This is the #1 feature request in motorcycle communities. MotoVault's advantage: ride mileage can auto-update the bike's odometer, triggering maintenance reminders at the right time. No ride-tracking app does this.

User Stories

As a solo rider, I want to set a safety contact who gets notified when I start and finish a ride, with live GPS if I don't check in.
As a touring rider, I want my ride distance to automatically update my bike's mileage so maintenance schedules stay accurate.
As a recreational rider, I want to save my favorite routes and see stats (distance, duration, elevation) for each ride.

Requirements

PriorityRequirement
P0Basic ride recording: start/stop, GPS track, distance, duration, max/avg speed
P0Safety contact: SMS/push notification on ride start, auto-alert if no activity for configurable timeout
P0Auto-mileage sync: ride distance added to motorcycle's odometer reading, triggering OEM schedule recalculation
P1Ride history with map view, searchable by date/bike/distance
P1Ride-linked expense logging: "Log fuel for this ride" prompt after completing a ride
P2Route sharing and discovery (community routes by region)
Target: 35% weekly active ride loggers within 90 days
Mileage accuracy: 85% of rides result in odometer update acceptance

Strategic value: Ride logging transforms MotoVault from "open it when something breaks" into "open it every time you ride." This is the single biggest lever for DAU/WAU metrics.

5
Seasonal Intelligence & Smart Reminders
High Retention Effort: Low

Problem: In northern climates, bikes sit for 3–5 months. Riders forget winterization steps (fuel stabilizer, battery tender, tire pressure) and spring prep (fluid check, chain lube, brake inspection). Your OEM schedules are mileage-based — they don't account for time-based degradation or seasonal transitions. Seasonal push notifications are the easiest way to re-engage dormant winter users.

User Stories

As a seasonal rider, I want a winterization checklist pushed to me in October and a spring-prep checklist in March, customized for my bike type and climate.
As a rider storing my bike, I want time-based reminders (e.g., "check battery tender connection" every 30 days during storage) even when I'm not riding.

Requirements

PriorityRequirement
P0User location/climate zone setting (or auto-detect from device)
P0Seasonal maintenance checklists: winterization + spring prep, generated per motorcycle type
P0Push notification schedule tied to seasonal transitions
P1AI-personalized seasonal tips based on bike type, riding frequency, and experience level
P2Weather-aware reminders: "Rain expected this week — check tire tread depth"
Target: 50% winter retention (vs. current assumed ~20%)
Checklist completion: 60% of seasonal checklists started

Why now: You already have push notification preferences in the mobile app, OEM schedules, and the AI insights system. This is mostly content + scheduling logic — 1–2 weeks of work with outsized retention impact during the deadest months of the year.

6
Mechanic Finder & Shop Integration
Revenue: B2B Channel Competitive Moat Effort: Medium

Problem: Finding a trustworthy motorcycle mechanic is one of the top frustrations for riders. Google Reviews don't differentiate between car and motorcycle work quality. Riders ask in forums, Facebook groups, and Reddit — always the same question: "Who's a good shop near [city]?" MotoVault can own this by connecting its maintenance data to local shops.

User Stories

As a rider needing professional service, I want to find motorcycle-specialized shops near me, rated by other MotoVault users, so I don't waste time with car-focused garages.
As a shop owner, I want riders to share their MotoVault maintenance history with me before an appointment so I can prepare and diagnose faster.
As a rider who just got a diagnostic result, I want to see "Shops near you that can fix this" with the ability to share my diagnostic report directly.

Requirements

PriorityRequirement
P0Curated motorcycle shop directory with location, specialties (sportbike, Harley, vintage, etc.), and user ratings
P0"Share with mechanic" button on diagnostic results and maintenance history (extends existing share link)
P1In-app booking request (send maintenance context + preferred time to shop)
P2Shop dashboard (web): mechanics can view shared bike histories, mark tasks complete, and push updates back to the rider's app
B2B target: 200 listed shops in top 20 metro areas within 6 months
Share-to-shop rate: 20% of diagnostics result in shop share

Revenue angle: This opens a B2B revenue stream. Shops pay for premium listings, booking integration, and access to MotoVault's rider base. This is how you build a two-sided marketplace without building a marketplace.

As a beginner rider, I want a structured learning path ("Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced") so I know what to learn next instead of browsing randomly.
As a competitive learner, I want badges and streaks for completing articles and quizzes so I feel rewarded for investing time in motorcycle knowledge.

Requirements

P0Achievement badges: "First Article Read," "Quiz Ace (100%)," "5-Day Streak," "Engine Expert (all engine articles)"

P2Community challenges: "This week: Complete the Tire Safety path" with leaderboard

Streak retention: 30% of users maintain 7-day streak
8
Pre-Ride Checklist (T-CLOCS Integration)
Retention: Daily Habit Effort: Very Low

Problem: You already have a T-CLOCS checklist on the marketing website (/tools/tclocs-checklist) — but it's not in the mobile app where riders actually need it. The MSF (Motorcycle Safety Foundation) T-CLOCS inspection is the gold standard for pre-ride safety checks. Making this a quick, tappable checklist linked to the rider's bike creates a daily habit touchpoint.

User Stories

As a safety-conscious rider, I want a quick pre-ride checklist on my phone so I can do a T-CLOCS inspection in under 2 minutes before every ride.
As a new rider, I want the checklist to explain each item (with photos or diagrams) so I know what I'm looking for.

Requirements

PriorityRequirement
P0T-CLOCS checklist in mobile app: Tires, Controls, Lights, Oil, Chassis, Stands — tappable items with checkmarks
P0Quick-launch from home screen (one tap to start checklist)
P1Customizable checklist per bike type (add/remove items)
P1Issue flagging: if a checklist item fails, prompt to create a maintenance task or start a diagnostic
P2Checklist history: "Last 10 pre-ride checks" with completion % trend
Target: 20% of riders use checklist weekly within 60 days
Diagnostic funnel: 10% of failed checklist items lead to diagnostic submission

Why now: This is a 1-week build that creates a daily engagement touchpoint and funnels users into your existing diagnostic and maintenance flows. It also reinforces MotoVault's brand positioning as a safety-first platform.

5. Adjustments to Existing Features

Beyond new features, there are high-impact improvements to what you already ship:

Expense Tracking

Current limitation: Flat list of expenses filtered by bike and year. No visualization, no aggregation, no insights.

Adjustment: Add expense categories for insurance and registration (two of the biggest annual costs riders track). Add a "recurring expense" type for monthly payments (loan, insurance). Integrate expense data into the Bike Passport for total cost of ownership visibility.

AI Diagnostics

Current limitation: One-shot diagnosis with no follow-up. No way to track if the issue was resolved, what the actual fix was, or how much it cost.

Adjustment: Add a "resolution" field to diagnostics — when a user completes a related maintenance task, prompt them to link it to a past diagnostic. This closes the loop and builds a ground-truth dataset. Also consider adding a "severity escalation" notification: if a user diagnosed something as "medium" 30 days ago and hasn't acted on it, send a reminder.

OEM Schedules

Current limitation: Mileage-interval only. Many critical services are time-based (brake fluid every 2 years, coolant every 3 years).

Adjustment: Add time-based intervals alongside mileage. Trigger whichever comes first. This is especially important for low-mileage riders and seasonal riders whose bikes age faster by calendar than by odometer.

Share Links

Current limitation: Functional but visually basic. Shows raw data without context.

Adjustment: Transform into the "Bike Passport" (Opportunity #2). Add a MotoVault branding layer, OEM compliance percentage, total investment figure, and QR code. This turns a utility feature into a marketing asset — every shared Passport is a MotoVault advertisement.

Onboarding

Current limitation: 17 screens is long. Onboarding asks about learning preferences, maintenance style, and spending — but the app doesn't visibly act on most of these signals beyond initial insights.

Adjustment: Reduce onboarding to 8–10 screens (bike details + experience level + goals). Move everything else to progressive disclosure — ask about maintenance style when they first visit the garage, learning preferences when they first visit the learn tab. Use the data you collect to personalize the home dashboard ("Your bike needs attention" vs. "Ready to ride" states).

"Bikes with complete service history" — defined as motorcycles in MotoVault with ≥80% of OEM-recommended tasks logged and current.
