2. **"motorcycle expenses" / "motorcycle costs"** — Fuelly dominates car fuel tracking but no one owns the moto expense category. Direct, high-intent, low difficulty.
3. **"trip planner" + "motorcycle"** — calimoto, Scenic, REVER own "navigation" / "GPS" but the literal phrase "trip planner" is less crowded. Position as planning, not navigation.

5. **"bike maintenance" cross-traffic** — Bicycle maintenance apps rank here. Motorcycle riders also search this term. Easy ranking, decent volume.

## Keywords to AVOID

- **"GPS"** — Dominated by Rever (14k reviews), Scenic (7k), Detecht (3k). You will not outrank them on this.
- **"navigation"** — Same. Too entrenched.
- **"OBD2" / "scanner"** — You don't have hardware integration. Don't claim it.
- **"track"** as standalone — Conflicts with race-track / fitness apps.
- **Brand names** (Harley, BMW, Yamaha, etc.) — Apple will reject keywords that infringe trademarks.
