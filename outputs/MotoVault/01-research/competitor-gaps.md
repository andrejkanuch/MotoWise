## Metadata patterns from top competitors

### Title structure
- "REVER - Motorcycle GPS & Rides" → brand + dash + 3 features
- "Detecht - Motorcycle App & GPS" → brand + dash + category claim
- "Scenic Motorcycle Navigation" → brand + 2 keyword
- "calimoto Motorcycle Navigation" → brand + 2 keyword
- "Fuelly: MPG & Service Tracker" → brand + colon + 3 features
- **MotoVault current:** "MotoVault - Bike Maintenance" → brand + dash + 2 keyword (good but "Bike" is ambiguous)

### Subtitle patterns
- Lead with the hero benefit
- Use "&" to chain 2-3 features
- Avoid duplicating words from the title

### Description openers
- All start with a hook: "Looking to elevate your riding experience..." (REVER), "Looking for an all-in-one motorcycle app..." (Detecht), "TAKE THE SCENIC ROUTE..." (Scenic), "Join over three million motorcyclists..." (calimoto)
- Pattern: hook → social proof → feature list with sub-headers

## Strategic positioning recommendation

**Position MotoVault as:** "The motorcycle owner's app — not just a GPS."

**Tagline angle:** Where REVER/Scenic/calimoto are about THE RIDE, MotoVault is about THE BIKE. Maintenance, costs, diagnostics, AND rides — the complete owner experience.

**Three positioning pillars:**
1. **Own your bike** — maintenance, service reminders, expenses
2. **Ride smarter** — trip planning, ride logging
3. **Fix it faster** — AI diagnostic (the moat)

This positioning lets you cohabit the App Store with REVER/Scenic without competing for the same searches. You're not "another GPS" — you're the bike management hub that also logs rides.
