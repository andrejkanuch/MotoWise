# MotoVault — Review Response Templates

**Generated:** 2026-04-11
**Tone:** Direct, friendly, rider-to-rider. No corporate-speak. First name basis.
**Rule:** Respond to EVERY 1-3 star review within 24 hours, every 4-5 star within 72 hours.

---

## Why responses matter

- Apple and Google both factor "developer responsiveness" into store ranking signals.
- Updating a review (replied-to negative reviews are 4x more likely to be edited upward).
- Other potential users read your responses — they're public.
- Rider community is tight; word travels.

## What NOT to do in review responses

- Don't argue with the reviewer publicly.
- Don't blame the user ("you should have...").
- Don't promise specific dates you can't keep.
- Don't paste the same template verbatim — personalize the first line at minimum.
- Don't mention "the algorithm" or "App Store ranking" — sounds desperate.
- Don't ask the reviewer to update their review. (Apple actively discourages this; Google tolerates it but it looks needy.) Earn the update by fixing the issue.
