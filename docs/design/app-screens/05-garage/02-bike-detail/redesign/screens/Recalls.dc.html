Engine may stall when wheelie control activates
Nov 21, 2024
A software error in the fuel-injection ECU may cause an engine stall or loss of power when the Wheelie Control feature is activated. Affects 2022 and 2024–2025 CRF1100.
Risk
A stall or loss of power can increase the risk of a crash.
Fix
Dealers reprogram the FI-ECU software, free of charge. Honda's number for this recall is KR1.
