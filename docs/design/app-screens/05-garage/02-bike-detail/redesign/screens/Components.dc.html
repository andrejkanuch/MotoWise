Four states, one anatomy: tag · title + due · mark-done circle (44 px, thumb side). Only an overdue Critical row gets a tinted background; an overdue Low keeps a plain card so the list stays ranked, not alarmed. History rows swap the tag for the date and the circle for the cost — full-contrast text, no strikethrough. In code the row is a View with two sibling Pressables (title block opens the task; the circle completes it) plus accessibilityActions “Mark done” and “Reorder” — never a button inside a link. Tap the circle = instant complete with today + current odometer, success haptic, 180 ms fill, then a 5 s snackbar “Done · Add details · Undo”. Swipe right also completes; swipe left reveals Edit · Delete; long-press opens a context menu with preview. In “My order” a grip appears left of the tag; the circle stays. Long-press (Android) or “Select” in the sort menu (iOS) enters multi-select.

Ride status is three words, not a percentage: Not ready = an overdue Critical task or an expired document the law requires (insurance, inspection, registration); Check before riding = an open recall, an overdue High, or a required document expiring within 30 days; Ready otherwise; a bike with nothing tracked shows neutral “Nothing tracked yet”. Overdue Low and Medium never change it. The card links to the top attention row. Attention rows rank: recalls → overdue Critical/High → expiring documents → other overdue → due-soon; max three, then a “N more” row. The Service badge counts only overdue Critical/High. A recall carries a per-rider state — open, fixed (date, shop, odometer, linked to history), not my VIN, or snoozed four weeks — so the status can return to Ready; open recalls that mention stall, brakes, steering, fuel or fire are CRIT, the rest HIGH.

A stat is eyebrow · mono value · one-line basis. The basis line is mandatory: it says what period and what denominator, so the three money figures on the bike page can never disagree silently. No cost-per-distance figure anywhere — riders do not think in money per kilometre. Stats are never tappable on their own; the card that holds them is.
Buttons
Text action
One primary per screen, always copper with dark ink (4.8:1 — white on copper fails). Secondary is the raised grey. Destructive is red text only, never a filled button, and lives at the bottom of a sheet or detail; tasks, notes and expenses delete with a 5 s Undo snackbar instead of a dialog, documents and the bike keep a typed confirm. The floating action sits above the tab bar, thumb side, with a 96 px list inset under it: labelled “Log” on Overview (it opens a chooser), icon-only on Service / Costs / Bike where it opens that segment’s form directly. Every control is at least 44 pt (48 dp on Android) with hitSlop on the small chips. Height 52 / 48 / 36 for primary / secondary / small; radius 14 on continuous curves.
