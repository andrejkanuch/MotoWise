Knowing how much your motorcycle actually costs to own and operate requires consistent tracking of fuel, maintenance, insurance, gear, and every other expense that comes with riding. While you could use a spreadsheet, purpose-built apps make the process faster, more insightful, and — critically — something you will actually stick with.

In this comparison, we evaluate the best motorcycle expense tracker apps available in 2026, examining their features, pricing, ease of use, and how well they serve the specific needs of motorcycle owners.

## What to Look for in a Motorcycle Expense Tracker

Before diving into specific apps, here are the key features that separate a good tracker from a great one:

- **Quick expense entry** — If it takes more than 30 seconds to log a fill-up or service, you will stop using it
- **Maintenance scheduling** — Reminders based on mileage or time intervals
- **Multi-vehicle support** — Many riders own more than one bike
- **Cost analysis** — Breakdowns by category, cost-per-mile calculations, trend visualization
- **Fuel economy tracking** — MPG calculations from fill-up data
- **Data export** — Ability to export records for tax purposes or when selling your bike
- **Offline functionality** — You should be able to log expenses without cell service
- **Motorcycle-specific features** — Chain maintenance, tire tracking, seasonal storage — not just generic vehicle fields

## Drivvo

### Overview

Drivvo is a popular general-purpose vehicle expense tracker with a large user base. It handles fuel tracking, maintenance records, and basic expense categorization for any type of vehicle.

### Strengths

**Mature and Stable.** Drivvo has been around for years and the core tracking functionality is polished and reliable.

**Strong Fuel Tracking.** Excellent fuel economy calculations with visualizations showing MPG trends over time. Supports partial fill-ups and multiple fuel types.

**Income Tracking.** Unique feature for riders who use their motorcycle for delivery or courier work — track earnings alongside expenses.

**Google Maps Integration.** Automatically calculate trip distances using GPS tracking.

### Weaknesses

- **Not motorcycle-specific.** Expense categories are designed for cars. There is no distinction between chain maintenance, brake pad types, or motorcycle-specific service items. You can create custom categories, but they require manual setup.
- **No maintenance intelligence.** Reminders are basic time/mileage counters without any smart scheduling.
- **Ad-supported free tier.** Ads can be disruptive during quick logging.
- **No diagnostic features.** Purely a tracking tool with no guidance on what maintenance means or when something might need attention.

### Pricing

Free with ads. Premium at $2.99/month removes ads and adds cloud backup, data export, and multi-device sync.

### Best For

Riders who want a proven, no-frills expense tracker and do not need motorcycle-specific features or AI-powered insights.

## Fuelio

### Overview

Fuelio started as a fuel-tracking app and has evolved to include maintenance and expense tracking. It is particularly popular among European users and has a strong focus on fuel economy data.

### Strengths

**Best-in-Class Fuel Tracking.** Fuelio's fuel logging and MPG calculation features are exceptionally detailed, with support for multiple fuel types, partial fill-ups, and cost-per-distance calculations in any unit system.

**One-Time Purchase.** Unlike most competitors, Fuelio's premium version is a one-time purchase rather than a subscription. For budget-conscious riders, this is appealing.

**Google Drive Backup.** Automatic backup to Google Drive means your data is safe without paying for cloud storage.

**Trip Computer.** GPS-based trip logging with automatic distance calculation.

### Weaknesses

- **Limited iOS support.** The Android version is fully featured; the iOS version lags behind significantly in features and updates.
- **Generic vehicle categories.** Like Drivvo, Fuelio treats all vehicles the same. Motorcycle-specific tracking requires manual category creation.
- **Basic maintenance reminders.** Simple interval-based reminders without smart scheduling.
- **Dated interface.** The UI, while functional, feels less modern than newer competitors.
- **No community or social features.** Purely a solo tracking experience.

### Pricing

Free with ads. Premium at $5.99 one-time removes ads and unlocks all features.

### Best For

Android users who prioritize fuel economy tracking and want a one-time purchase rather than a subscription. Riders who are comfortable with a generic platform.

## Moto Shed

### Overview

Moto Shed is a motorcycle-specific app designed by riders for riders. It focuses on garage management, maintenance tracking, and community features.

### Strengths

**Motorcycle-Specific Design.** Like MotoVault, Moto Shed understands that motorcycles are not cars. The interface and categories reflect motorcycle ownership.

**Garage Feature.** Visually showcase your motorcycle collection with photos, specs, and modification lists. Good for riders who enjoy documenting their builds.

**Community.** Connect with other riders, share garage photos, and discuss maintenance topics within the app.

**Modification Tracking.** Track aftermarket parts and modifications separately from maintenance — useful for documenting a build.

### Weaknesses

- **Weaker expense analytics.** Cost tracking exists but the analysis tools are basic compared to dedicated trackers. Limited visualization and no cost-per-mile calculations.
- **No AI features.** Maintenance reminders are manual rather than intelligent.
- **Smaller development team.** Updates are less frequent than larger competitors.
- **Community focus can overshadow utility.** Some users report that the social features feel more prominent than the tracking tools.

### Pricing

Free tier with limited features. Premium at $3.99/month for full maintenance tracking, unlimited garage entries, and ad removal.

### Best For

Riders who value community features and garage documentation alongside basic maintenance and expense tracking.
