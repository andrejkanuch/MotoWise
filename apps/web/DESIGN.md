# Design System: MotoVault Web — Trackside

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Trackside"**

The web is the Race Plate world (`apps/mobile/DESIGN.md`) seen from the edge of the track: a full-bleed ride photograph carries the emotion, and everything else speaks in the plate's voice. The one object the site shares with the app is the Bike Plate, a rounded panel whose fill is a bike's readiness, drawn exactly as the app draws it, including on the home hero.

Around the plate the site is quiet and mechanical. A graphite ramp with bone ink is the default; a warm paper ramp with near-black ink is the light scheme, and both are first-class. Barlow Condensed carries headlines and numbers; every sentence, label and button is the system face. Copper is reserved for the act: the primary button, the link, the current selection. Content sits on the page, separated by spacing and hairlines rather than cards, and surfaces lift by stepping up the ramp rather than by casting shadows.

The world retires the earlier editorial web (serif display, mono labels, warm-gradient copper, eyebrows above titles) and borrows nothing from it. The livery stripe on the plate is the one diagonal on any viewport.

**Key Characteristics:**
- One token source: `packages/design-system/src/mv-tokens.css`. Dark on `:root`, light on `html:not(.dark)`; the theme provider toggles the class and a pre-paint script resolves storage, then OS preference.
- The Bike Plate is the signature; its triad colours appear nowhere else.
- Condensed numerals and headlines against a native system face.
- Flat and tonal: no card shadows, blur budget spent on the navbar only.
- Hairline rows and spec tables instead of cards; at most one card-styled element per section.
- Motion is visible-by-default and transform-only; one authored moment per page.

## Colors

A graphite-and-bone instrument palette (paper-and-ink in light), one warm action colour, and a three-step state triad that belongs to the plate.

### Primary
- **Exhaust Copper** (`exhaust-copper` dark, `light-copper` light): fills the primary button, the focus ring (`--mv-focus`), the field caret, the selected chip border and the plate's livery stripe. Hover steps to `copper-hover` / `light-copper-hover`. Ink on copper is `on-copper-dark` (graphite) in dark and white in light.
- **Copper Text** (`copper-text` dark, `light-copper-text` light): links (`.mv-link`, underlined at `copper-border`), row hover, footer link hover, selected chip label.
- **Copper Tint** (`--mv-copper-tint`, copper at 10%; strong 16%): tap highlight and selected backgrounds only.

### Plate-state triad
- **Plate Bone** (`plate-bone`) ready, **Signal Yellow** (`plate-signal`) due soon, **Plate Red** (`plate-red`) overdue. Same fills in both schemes, always printed in `plate-ink`.
- As text on the ramp the triad uses its inks: `due-ink-dark` / `light-due-ink`, `overdue-ink-dark` / `light-overdue-ink`. `overdue-ink` is also the field-error and alert icon ink.

### Neutral
- **Graphite ramp** (`graphite-ground` → `graphite-card` → `graphite-raised` → `graphite-option` → `graphite-line-step`): page ground, card, raised controls (ghost button, field, chip, segmented track), selected option, the visible line step. `graphite-ground-deep` (`--mv-bg-2`) is a web-only deeper step for alternating bands and the hero's bottom fade.
- **Paper ramp** (`paper-ground` → `paper-card` white → `paper-raised` → `paper-option` → `paper-line-step`): the same roles in light. Light is a separate ramp, not an inversion.
- **Bone ink / light ink** (`ink` → `ink-4`): primary text, secondary text and ledes, meta and captions, glyphs and chevrons. `ink-4` is held at the 3:1 glyph floor; it is not a text colour.
- **Lines**: `line` (9% / 10%) for hairline rows and rules, `line-2` fainter, `field-line` (38% / 46%) for input borders at 3:1. Under `prefers-contrast: more` lines step up to 30% / 18% / 60% ink; under forced colours they become `CanvasText` and focus becomes `Highlight`.
- **Go / Info** (`go-green`, `info-blue`; light `light-go`, `light-info`): success and informational marks. Not part of the triad.
- **Danger tint / border**: the alert surface, always with overdue ink, never copper.

### Sanctioned exceptions
- **QR tile** (`qr-tile` with `plate-ink`): fixed in every scheme for scan reliability.
- **Google "G" mark**: the official four brand colours, never recoloured.
- **Dark scene**: a band laid over a dark photograph (home hero, closing CTA, trip heroes with a map) keeps the graphite tokens under the light scheme via `.mv-dark-scene`; the navbar joins the scene while it floats unscrolled over such a hero. Read the band's edge colour from outside the scene so it blends into the page ground.
- **Print**: the paper ramp applies regardless of scheme; plate and primary button keep their fills.

### Named Rules
**The Copper Is a Verb Rule.** Copper marks a primary action, a link or the current selection. Never status, never a heading accent, never gradient text, never a glow (no copper box-shadow). The livery stripe is the plate's own device, not decoration.

**The Triad Belongs to State Rule.** Bone, signal and red appear only inside depictions of the app: the Bike Plate and app screenshots.

**The Both-Schemes Rule.** Every colour is a `var(--mv-*)` with a value in both schemes. Text inks clear 4.5:1 on ground, card and raised in each; due/overdue/go shift to darker inks in light.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Barlow Condensed 500/600/700 via `next/font` (`--font-plate`), fallback Arial Narrow
**Body Font:** the system stack (`-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, Segoe UI, Roboto, Helvetica Neue, Arial`)
**Mono:** `ui-monospace, SF Mono, Menlo, Consolas` for genuine code and data only

**Character:** Plate lettering for what a rider scans (titles, odometers, costs) against a native face for what a rider reads.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (700, `clamp(56px, min(9.6vw, 18vh), 150px)`, 0.94, max 14ch): the home hero headline only, two forced lines.
- **Headline** (700, `clamp(44px, 5.5vw, 84px)`, 0.96): the page H1 on Persuade pages (`.mv-section-title`). Read pages use `clamp(38px, 5.4vw, 62px)`; Operate pages (auth, garage, share) use a fixed 44px.
- **Section** (700, `clamp(34px, 4.2vw, 56px)`, 0.98): the Persuade H2 (`.mv-h2`). Read and Operate H2 is 34px / 1.05 / 600.
- **Title** (600, 26px, 1.1): H3 for sub-sections, sheets and cards (`.mv-h3`).
- **Operate head** (system 600, 20px / 1.25): in-page section heads on signed-in and auth pages.
- **Figures** (600, tabular): plate 64/64 hero (56 at ≤480), 40/42 compact, then 28 and 20/22. A unit beside a figure is display 500 at 0.5× in `ink-2` (`.mv-unit`).
- **Lede** (400, 18px / 1.55, −0.01em, max 52ch) and the hero description (17px / 1.55, max 440px).
- **Body** (400, 17px / 1.6, max 60ch) on Read pages; **compact** 16px / 1.55 for rows and inputs (inputs never below 16px).
- **Small** (15px): the workhorse step: buttons (600), nav links, chips, segmented options, row subtitles, tables, alerts, footer links.
- **Meta** (500, 13px / 18px, `ink-3`): crumbs, captions, footer column titles, hints, errors, trust line. 13 is the floor; 12 and 14 are not steps.

### Named Rules
**The Condensed Means Number Rule.** `--mv-font-display` sets H1, H2, H3 and figures, with tabular numerals for every figure, table cell and row aside. Every sentence, label and button is `--mv-font-body`.

**The Plain Voice Rule.** Sentence case everywhere, headings and buttons included. No eyebrows or kickers above titles, no section numbers, no letter-spaced uppercase, no italic display, no serif, no mono labels. A status line sits under a title, never above it.

## Layout

One container: `--mv-container` 1240px, with the gutter carried inside it so the hero H1, every section H2, the navbar brand and the footer brand share one left edge. Gutters step 40 → 24 (≤900px) → 20 (≤480px); the navbar respects safe-area insets.

Section rhythm comes from the shells: weight sections 160 (hero top), 120 inner, 96 stats; at ≤900 they become 128/96/64, at ≤480 120/96/48. Read pages run a 760px column (index pages 920px, wide pages the container) at 120 → 96 vertical padding, with prose at 60ch and ledes at 52ch. Fixed intervals: crumbs → H1 40, H1 → lede 28, lede → meta 20. The two-column grid is `minmax(0, 1fr)` twice at a 64 gap, collapsing to one column at 48 below 900.

Spacing values observed in the build: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 96, 120, 160 (12 for row padding and button rows, 20 as the phone gutter, 40 as the desktop gutter and crumbs interval). Left-align headers and body; only a final CTA may centre.

Every interactive target is at least 44px: buttons, icon buttons, nav links, crumb links (via negative margin on 13px text), footer links (11px vertical padding), and chips and segments under a coarse pointer. The navbar is 72px resting and 64px scrolled, published once as `--mv-nav-h` for anything that sticks beneath it; the cookie banner publishes `--mv-cookie-banner-h` for any fixed bottom bar.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat and tonal. A surface lifts by moving one step up the ramp (ground → card → raised → option); ghost buttons, fields, chips and the segmented track sit on the raised step, and a selected segment one step above that. Cards and rows carry no drop shadow.

The only blur is the scrolled navbar: `navbar-glass` (raised step at 92%) with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a hairline bottom border. With the menu open, or over a dark hero in light, the bar turns opaque ground.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Keyline ring** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px var(--mv-line)`): the phone mockup's edge.
- **Plate keyline** (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 2px var(--mv-plate-ink)`): the Bike Plate on light grounds, drawn above the stripe; removed inside a dark scene.
- **Active-tab rule** (`box-shadow: inset 0 -2px var(--mv-ink)`): the trip tab underline.

### Named Rules
**The Ramp Is the Shadow Rule.** No drop shadows on cards or rows; no blur outside the navbar.

## Shapes

Role-based radii: progress tracks 2, small marks (≤32px, app icon in brand, checkboxes, kbd) 6, controls and buttons 12 (segments inside a track 10), cards 16, the plate 20, frameless screenshot crops 28, the screen inside a device frame 36, the device frame 44 (38 / 34 at smaller widths, screen concentric at frame minus 8), pills fully round.

The only diagonal is the livery stripe: a copper band and a half-width ground-colour band rotated 45° through the plate's top-right corner (compact 140 × 9, hero 220 × 14). Everything else is orthogonal. Hairlines are 1px; a list rules its rows and drops its top rule when the element above already rules.

## Components

### Bike Plate (signature)
The bike's readiness as an object (`<BikePlate>`, `.mv-plate*`).
- **Anatomy:** figure row (plate figure plus a display-500 unit, baseline aligned); one caption line (15/20 500, hero 17/22 600); identity edge under a 1px rule at 16% plate ink: bold tabular racing number, then "make model · year" at 68% ink, one line, truncating. No kicker above the figure.
- **States:** `ready` / `due` / `overdue` set the fill; ink is always `plate-ink`; the overdue identity line keeps full ink. A screen-reader-only state word follows the caption.
- **Sizes:** compact (16/20/14 padding, min 264px, figure 40) and hero (20/24/18, min 360px, figure 64; full width at ≤480).
- **Light grounds:** a 2px plate-ink keyline. Inside a card host the stripe's ground band reads `--mv-plate-ground`.
- **Selection** inverts the plate (ink ground, bone text).

**The One Signature Motion.** `data-settle` makes the plate settle (scale 0.98 → 1 over `dur-3`, expo ease) and, 120ms in, the livery stripe slides along its own 45° axis into place over `dur-4` (420ms). Transform only and clipped by the plate, so a stalled frame is a plate without its stripe, never a missing plate. `settle="late"` adds 240ms so the home hero reads photo settles, then stripe lays. One per page family, never inside `.mv-reveal`; used on the home hero and the Pro checkout success page. A state change cross-fades the fill over `dur-3`.

### Buttons
- **Shape:** control radius (12px), 1px border always part of the box so primary and ghost match to the pixel. Heights: 44 default, 52 block (`.mv-btn-block`), 40 compact.
- **Primary:** copper fill, on-copper ink, 15px / 600 system face, 12 × 22 padding, 10px icon gap. Hover: copper-hover and a 1px lift. Active: scale 0.985 with a 60ms response.
- **Ghost:** raised step, ink text, line border; hover steps to the option surface and line-step border.
- **Disabled:** primary at 50% opacity; ghost in ink-3. Busy shows a progress cursor.
- **Icon button:** 44px square, transparent, ink-2; hover fills `--mv-fill` and goes to ink. The theme toggle renders both sun and moon and cross-fades them with a quarter turn.
- **Link:** copper-text, 500, 1px underline at 3px offset in copper-border; hover to copper-hover with a full-colour underline. `is-quiet` drops the underline until hover.

### Chips and segmented control
- **Chip:** raised step, line border, ink-2, 15px / 500, height 40 (44 tall or coarse pointer), radius 12. Selected: copper border, copper-text label, background unchanged.
- **Segmented:** a raised track with 2px padding; options 40 high, 15px / 500 ink-2; the selected option steps to the option surface with ink. Selection is a surface, never copper. Under forced colours a selected chip or segment gains a 2px outline.

### Hairline rows and spec tables
- **Rows (`.mv-rows`):** 1px line between rows, 12px vertical padding, min 44px; title 16/500, subtitle 15 ink-3, aside 15 ink-3 or a 20px figure in ink-2. A navigating row turns copper-text on hover and nudges its chevron 2px; a disclosure row lifts to ink.
- **Spec table (`.mv-table`):** 15px, 14 × 12 cells flush at the edges, line rules, header in ink-3 / 500 over a line-step rule, row headers in ink. No zebra, no card.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** raised step, `field-line` border, radius 12, height 48 (52 for phone auth), 14px side padding, 16px text, copper caret, ink-3 placeholder.
- **Label** 15 / 500 ink-2 eight above; **hint** 13/18 ink-3; **error** 13 overdue-ink, entering with a 4px drop.
- **Focus:** copper border plus one 2px focus ring at zero offset. Hover: line-step border. Invalid: overdue-ink border.
- **Alert:** danger tint, danger border, radius 12, 14 × 16 padding, 15px ink text, overdue-ink icon.

### Navigation
- **Navbar:** fixed, transparent over the hero at 72px; scrolled it becomes the 64px glass bar. Brand 17 / 600 with a 32px icon at radius 6. Links 15 / 500 ink-2 at 44px, hover to ink on `--mv-fill`.
- **Breadcrumbs:** 13 / 500 ink-3, chevrons in ink-4, current page in ink, truncating at 42ch, 40 above the H1.
- **Footer:** 2.5fr brand column plus three link columns (two columns at ≤820), 13px column titles in sentence case, 15px ink-2 links at 44px rows, a meta-step bottom bar, and an outlined condensed wordmark (1px line-step stroke, aria-hidden) as decoration.

### Phone mockup and loader
- **Phone mockup:** 320px (260 / 220), 9:19.5, raised step, 8px padding, keyline ring. At most one full device frame per page; other screenshots are cropped at radius 28.
- **Loader:** a 2px copper arc over a line ring with a dashed tread and a pulsing copper hub (44 / 80 / 140). It is an activity indicator, the one sanctioned loop; reduced motion stops it and the dots become an ellipsis.

### Motion kit
Easing `--mv-ease` `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)` for state, `--mv-ease-expo` `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` for entrances. Durations: `dur-1` 160ms colour, `dur-2` 200ms translate/scale/opacity, `dur-3` 240ms height, disclosure and the plate settle, `dur-4` 420ms the livery stripe only.
- **Reveal:** `.mv-reveal` rises 18px with opacity over 700ms on the scroll timeline (`entry 0%–30%`), only where `animation-timeline: view()` is supported; content is visible by default. One or two per page.
- **Enter utilities:** `.mv-enter-down` / `-up` / `-list` start from `@starting-style` (6–8px offset; list staggers 30ms, capped at 210ms).
- **Disclosure:** chevron rotates 180° (plus mark 45°), `::details-content` animates height over `dur-3`.
- **Hero photo:** scales 1.06 → 1 once over 700ms.
- **Theme change:** a view transition cross-fade over `dur-2`, user click only, skipped under reduced motion.
- **Reduced motion:** entrances, settles, nudges and lifts go to none; colour transitions stay.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** read every colour, radius, font, easing and duration from `var(--mv-*)` in `mv-tokens.css`, and check the result in dark and light.
- **Do** show readiness with `<BikePlate>` (triad fill, plate ink), and give it the settle on at most one plate per page family.
- **Do** use copper only for the primary action, a link, the focus ring or the current selection, with `on-copper` as its ink.
- **Do** set H1/H2/H3 and every figure in Barlow Condensed with tabular numerals, and every sentence, label and button in the system face.
- **Do** snap type to the steps above (13 floor, 15 workhorse, 16 inputs) and spacing to 4–160 as listed.
