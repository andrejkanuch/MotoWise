## 2. Opus.pro Setup — Getting More From Automated Posting

You're smart to automate since you're a solo dev. But there's a difference between *posting daily* and *posting strategically*. Here's how to get much more from OpusClip:

### Optimal Posting Frequency

- **TikTok:** 1–2 posts/day is great. The algorithm rewards consistency and gives small accounts a fair shot on the For You Page.
- **Instagram:** 4–7 Reels/week (not daily — quality over quantity). Mix in 2–3 carousels/week if possible.

### Content Settings in Opus.pro

- **Clip length:** Aim for **15–30 seconds** as your default. Shorter clips (7–15s) for pure entertainment, longer (30–45s) for educational/tutorial content. Avoid 60s+ unless the content is exceptionally engaging.
- **Captions/subtitles:** **Always on.** The majority of viewers watch without sound. Burned-in captions from OpusClip are better than platform auto-captions for branding consistency.
- **Hook detection:** OpusClip has AI hook detection — enable it. The first 3 seconds determine whether people scroll past or watch. Up to 50% of viewers drop off in the first 3 seconds.
- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 vertical for both platforms.
- **Cross-posting:** Use OpusClip's scheduling to post at different times on each platform (don't post simultaneously — stagger by 2–4 hours).

### Source Content Strategy

This is where most automated accounts fail. If you're just clipping random motorcycle videos, you'll get generic engagement. Instead, be intentional about your source material:

**Best source content to feed into Opus.pro:**
1. **Your own screen recordings** — Record 5–10 minute walkthroughs of MotoVault scanning a bike, reading error codes, tracking maintenance schedules. OpusClip will cut these into multiple short clips. This is your highest-value content because it's unique and nobody else has it.
2. **Maintenance & diagnostics long-form** — Find (or create) long-form content about motorcycle error codes, common mechanical failures, seasonal maintenance checklists. Clips from these perform extremely well because riders actively search for this info.
3. **"What went wrong" content** — Repair shop horror stories, costly mistakes, warning signs riders ignore. These have massive engagement because fear of expensive repairs is a powerful motivator.

**What to avoid clipping:**
- Low-resolution source material
- Content that's already been widely clipped by others
- Pure entertainment with no connection to your brand

## 3. Content Strategy — What to Post (and Why)

### The 70/20/10 Rule for MotoVault

| % of posts | Type | Purpose |
|-----------|------|---------|
| **70%** | Value content | Maintenance how-tos, error code explainers, "check this before every ride", common failure points |
| **20%** | Product content | MotoVault diagnostics in action, app scanning a real bike, AI catching a problem |
| **10%** | Trend/viral | Trending sounds, moto memes, reaction videos, challenges |

### Content Pillars (Repeat These Weekly)

**Pillar 1: Maintenance & Diagnostics Knowledge**
"Your bike is showing this error code — here's what it actually means" / "5 things to check before every season" / "This $3 part saves your engine"
— Positions MotoVault as the go-to authority on motorcycle health. Algorithm gold because it's practical, searchable, and shareable.

**Pillar 2: AI Diagnostics in Action**
Screen recordings of MotoVault scanning a real bike, catching a problem, explaining an error code. "My bike threw a code — I scanned it and here's what MotoVault found."
— This is your conversion content. Keep it raw and real, not polished. Show the actual app workflow.

**Pillar 3: "What Went Wrong" Stories**
"This rider ignored this warning light — here's what happened" / "The most expensive maintenance mistake" / Breakdowns, fails, costly repairs that could have been prevented.
— Fear of expensive repairs is a massive motivator. These clips naturally lead to "there's an app that catches this early."

**Pillar 4: Engagement + Community**
"What's the first thing you check on a used bike?" / "Rate this garage setup" / "Most reliable bike under $5K?" / Polls, duets, stitches.
— Drives comments and builds your community of bike owners who care about maintenance.

### TikTok-Specific Tips

- **Use trending sounds** — even under clips. TikTok's algorithm boosts content using trending audio.
- **Text overlays** — Put the hook as text on screen in the first frame. "Most riders don't know this..."
- **Hashtags:** Use 3–5 targeted hashtags: `#biketok` `#motorcyclemaintenance` `#motovault` `#bikediagnostics` `#bikelife`. Don't spam 20+ hashtags.
- **Post timing:** Test posting between 7–9 AM and 6–9 PM in your target timezone. TikTok rewards early engagement velocity.
- **Reply to comments with video** — This is one of the highest-performing content types on TikTok and costs you almost no time.

### Instagram-Specific Tips

- **Carousels still work** — "5 things every sportbike rider should check monthly" as a swipeable carousel gets high save rates.
- **Reels cover image** — Design a consistent cover with your brand colors and a bold text title. This makes your grid look professional.
- **Stories** — Post 2–3 stories/day (even simple polls, "this or that" bike comparisons). Stories keep you visible and drive profile visits.
- **Hashtags:** 5–10 max. Mix broad (`#motorcycle`) with niche (`#motorcyclemaintenance` `#bikediagnostics` `#motorcyclerepair`).
- **Collab posts** — Use Instagram's Collab feature to co-post with motorcycle creators. Their followers see your content natively.

## 4. Making Your Profiles Stand Out — The "Brand Feel"

### Visual Consistency

- **Pick 2–3 brand colors** and use them in every thumbnail, text overlay, and story. If MotoVault has brand colors, use them.
- **Consistent caption style** on OpusClip — same font, same position, same colors across all clips.
- **TikTok profile video:** TikTok lets you set a profile video instead of a photo. Use a 3-second loop showing the app or a bike revving — it's eye-catching and most accounts don't use this.

### Bio Credibility Signals

What makes a bio feel "reliable" and trustworthy:

1. **Clarity** — State exactly what you are in one line. "AI motorcycle diagnostics" is better than "The future of riding."
2. **Social proof** — If you have any numbers, use them: "10K+ riders" / "4.8★ on App Store" / "Used in 40+ countries"
3. **Consistency across platforms** — Same name, same logo, same messaging on TikTok and Instagram. People cross-check.
4. **Professional link** — A Linktree/Beacons page with your app download, not a raw URL.
5. **Verified badge** — Apply for verification on both platforms once you have consistent posting history.

## 6. Key Metrics to Track (Ignore Vanity Numbers)

Don't obsess over follower count. Focus on:

| Metric | Why It Matters | Target |
|--------|---------------|--------|
| **Watch completion rate** | Algorithm's #1 signal | > 50% on 15s clips |
| **Saves & shares** | Indicates real value | Growing week-over-week |
| **Profile visits** | Shows curiosity about your brand | Track in analytics |
| **Link clicks** | Actual conversion to app | This is your north star |
| **Comments** | Community engagement | Reply to every one |

TikTok's average engagement rate is ~3.85% (nearly 8x higher than Instagram), and small accounts under 5K followers often see 4.2%+ engagement. The algorithm genuinely levels the playing field — your 2K-follower account can outperform a 200K account with better content.

## 7. What NOT to Do

- **Don't post the exact same clip to both platforms at the same time.** Stagger by hours and slightly vary captions.
- **Don't use 20+ hashtags.** It looks spammy and doesn't help. 3–5 on TikTok, 5–10 on Instagram.
- **Don't chase trends that don't fit your niche.** A random dance trend on a motorcycle app account confuses the algorithm about who to show your content to.
- **Don't ignore comments.** The algorithm rewards accounts that generate conversations. Reply to everything, especially in the first hour.
- **Don't make every post a sales pitch.** The 70/20/10 rule exists for a reason. Lead with value, not "download our app."
- **Don't use watermarked content from other platforms.** TikTok deprioritizes content with Instagram watermarks and vice versa.
